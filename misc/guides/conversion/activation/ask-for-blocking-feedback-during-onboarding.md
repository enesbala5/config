# Ask what is blocking users during onboarding

**Feeds stage:** audit

Build a feedback loop into the flow: a blocking question ("What's stopping
you?") at the point of friction, in-app thumbs up/down on features, and NPS
asked at a deliberate moment with the follow-up branched by score. Collect the
"why" after the score, not in the initial prompt, and time surveys so they
interrupt after value rather than before it.

Test survey timing and whether a small incentive changes response quality.
