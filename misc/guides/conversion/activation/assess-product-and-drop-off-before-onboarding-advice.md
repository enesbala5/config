# Establish product, activation and drop-off context before advising

**Feeds stage:** audit

Before recommending onboarding changes, establish three things: product context
(B2B or B2C, core value proposition), the activation definition (the aha moment
and the action that signals it), and the current state (what happens after
signup and where users drop off). Then ask for the activation rate target and
any cohort comparison of successful versus churned users.

Recommendations without this context cannot say what "successful onboarding"
means for this product.
