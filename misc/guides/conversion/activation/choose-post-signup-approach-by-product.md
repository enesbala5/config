# Choose the post-signup approach by product type

**Feeds stage:** audit

Pick the first-30-seconds approach from the product, not from habit:

- Product-first — simple, B2C or mobile products; risk: blank-slate overwhelm.
- Guided setup — products needing personalisation; risk: friction before value.
- Value-first — products with demo data; risk: the experience may not feel real.

Whichever you choose, give a clear single next action, no dead ends, and
progress indication if the flow is multi-step.
