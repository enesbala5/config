# Define "stalled" and give returners a short path back

**Feeds stage:** audit

Define stall criteria explicitly (days inactive, incomplete setup) instead of
waiting for churn. Recover in three ways: an email that reminds of value,
addresses blockers and offers help; an in-app "welcome back" that resumes where
the user left off; and personal outreach for high-value accounts.

Test re-engagement timing, a simplified path for returners, asking what is
blocking them, a demo offer, and a fresh-start or "what changed while you were
away" option.
