# Find the biggest drop-off and fix that step first

**Feeds stage:** audit

Map the onboarding funnel step by step — Signup → Step 1 → Step 2 → Activation
→ Retention — and put the conversion percentage at each stage. Focus effort on
the largest drop rather than the step that is easiest to change.

Without per-step data you cannot tell whether the problem is setup, activation
or return.
