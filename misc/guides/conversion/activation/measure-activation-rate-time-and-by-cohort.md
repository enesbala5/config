# Measure activation by rate, time, steps and cohort

**Feeds stage:** audit

Track four activation metrics: percentage of signups who reach the activation
event, time to activation, steps to activation, and activation split by cohort
or source. Broaden the picture with step completion rate, return rate and
feature adoption.

For onboarding experiments, measure the same way: activation rate, time to
activation, step completion, drop-off points, return rate, day 1/7/30
retention, feature adoption and support-request volume.
