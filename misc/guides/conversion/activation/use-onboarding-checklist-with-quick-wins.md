# Use a short, value-ordered onboarding checklist

**Feeds stage:** audit

Use a checklist when setup has multiple steps, several features to discover, or
a self-serve B2B audience. Keep it to 3–7 items, order by impact, start with
quick wins, show a progress bar and celebrate completion.

Always offer a dismiss option: a checklist that cannot be closed traps the
user. Test length (3–5 versus 5–7 items) rather than assuming a longer list is
more thorough.
