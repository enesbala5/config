# Test paywall headlines, CTAs and objection handling

**Feeds stage:** verify

Headlines: benefit-focused ("Unlock unlimited projects") versus feature-focused ("Get Pro features"), question versus statement, urgency versus value framing, personalised with usage data, and social proof ("Join 10,000+ Pro users").

CTAs: "Start Free Trial" versus "Upgrade Now" versus "Continue with Pro"; first person versus second person; value-specific ("Unlock Unlimited") versus generic ("Upgrade"); price in the CTA versus a separate price display.

Objection handling: a money-back guarantee, "Cancel anytime" placed prominently, an FAQ on the paywall, copy that answers the objection specific to the gated feature, and a chat or support option. Only test urgency framing when the urgency is genuine.
