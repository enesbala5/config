# Test frequency caps and dismiss behaviour

**Feeds stage:** verify

Frequency levers: prompts per session, cool-down length (hours versus days), escalating urgency versus consistent messaging, one prompt per feature versus consolidated prompts, and re-show rules after a major engagement milestone.

Dismiss levers: "Maybe later" versus "No thanks" versus "Remind me tomorrow", asking for a reason, offering an alternative (lower tier or annual discount), an exit survey, and friendly versus neutral decline copy. Track annoyance signals so a test that lifts clicks without driving long-term disengagement is not mistaken for a win.
