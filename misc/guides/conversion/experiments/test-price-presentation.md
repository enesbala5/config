# Test price presentation levers

**Feeds stage:** verify

Treat pricing presentation as a set of testable levers rather than a fixed
choice: annual versus monthly display with savings highlighted, price points,
"Most Popular" badge, number of visible tiers, plan ordering for anchoring, and
custom enterprise tier versus "Contact Sales". For hard-to-choose visitors,
test a pricing calculator, a guided flow or a plan recommendation quiz.

On in-app paywalls, also test price-per-day framing ("less than a coffee"),
showing the price after the trial versus emphasising "Start Free", prominent
versus deferred price display, a one-time purchase option alongside the
subscription, and discount structures — first period, limited-time countdown,
loyalty based on free usage, annual bundle and referral.
