# Test trial structure and expiration handling

**Feeds stage:** verify

Trial structure levers: 7 versus 14 versus 30 days, credit card required or not, full access versus limited features, an extension offer for engaged users, and a second trial for expired or churned users.

Expiration levers: countdown always visible versus only near the end, reminder email frequency and timing, a grace period versus immediate downgrade, a "last chance" discount, and a pause option versus immediate cancellation. Test these as changes to the offer, not as pressure tactics — say clearly what is lost and what is kept when the trial ends.
