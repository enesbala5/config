# Let email support in-app onboarding, not duplicate it

**Feeds stage:** plan

Coordinate email onboarding with the in-app flow: email should reinforce the
steps the product already teaches, not repeat them. Duplicating the in-app
sequence wastes the email and leaves the user unsure which path to follow.
Drive the reader back to the product with one specific CTA, and personalise the
message based on actions the user has already taken — or not taken.
