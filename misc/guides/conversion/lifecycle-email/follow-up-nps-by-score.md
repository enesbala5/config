# Follow up NPS responses by score

**Feeds stage:** plan

Keep the initial NPS email to the single question, collect the "why" in a
follow-up form, then branch: promoters (9-10) get a thank-you and a review or
referral ask, passives (7-8) get asked what would make it a 10, and detractors
(0-6) get personal outreach to understand the problem. Asking everyone the same
follow-up wastes the signal the score provides.
