# Prefer triggered emails to time-based sends

**Feeds stage:** plan

Action-based sends are more relevant than time-based ones: trigger on a feature
used, a milestone hit or a period of inactivity rather than only on a calendar
date. Relevance at the moment of behaviour beats a perfectly timed but generic
blast.

An onboarding trigger set usually mixes both: an immediate welcome, incomplete
onboarding at 24h and 72h, an activation celebration with a next step, and
feature discovery at days 3, 7 and 14. Even when a time gate decides when a
message goes, behaviour should decide what it says.
