# Audit the whole program against a category checklist

**Feeds stage:** plan

Use a six-category checklist to find gaps: onboarding (new users, new customers,
step reminders, invites), retention (upgrade, review, support, usage reports,
NPS, referral), billing (switch to annual, failed payment, cancellation survey,
renewal reminders), usage (summaries, events, milestones), win-back (expired
trials, cancelled customers) and campaigns (newsletter, seasonal, product
updates, pricing changes). Missing categories are program gaps, not individual
copy problems.
