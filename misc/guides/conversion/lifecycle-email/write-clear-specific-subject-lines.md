# Write clear, specific subject lines

**Feeds stage:** plan

Prefer clear over clever and specific over vague; aim for 40-60 characters and
lead with a benefit or genuine curiosity. Working patterns include a question
("Still struggling with X?"), a how-to ("How to [outcome] in [timeframe]"), a
number ("3 ways to [benefit]"), a direct name ("[First name], your [thing] is
ready"), and a story tease ("The mistake I made with [topic]"). Test emoji —
they polarise audiences.
