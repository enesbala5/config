# Reject paywall dark patterns

**Feeds stage:** verify

Do not hide the close button, make plan selection confusing, or use guilt-trip copy to push an upgrade. These tactics manufacture a decision instead of earning one, and trade long-term trust for a short-term click.

A paywall should be honest about what is paid, what happens on decline and what the user is agreeing to. If the upgrade needs deception to work, the offer or its timing is wrong.
