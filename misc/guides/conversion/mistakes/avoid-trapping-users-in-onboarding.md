# Do not trap users in onboarding

**Feeds stage:** verify

Anti-patterns to reject: dead ends after signup, multi-step flows with no
dismiss option, tours that cannot be closed or that reappear for returning
users, checklists that must be finished before the product is usable, and long
setup that puts friction before any value.

Every gated step needs a skip or exit: a user who cannot leave the flow will
leave the product.
