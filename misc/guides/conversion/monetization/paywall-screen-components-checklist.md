# Build the paywall screen from the seven components

**Feeds stage:** audit

A complete paywall screen has seven parts, each doing a job:

1. a headline focused on the gain — "Unlock [Feature] to [Benefit]";
2. a value demonstration — a preview, before/after or "with Pro you could…";
3. a feature comparison highlighting key differences with the current plan marked;
4. clear, simple pricing with annual and monthly options;
5. social proof — customer quotes or "X teams use this";
6. a specific, value-oriented CTA — "Start Getting [Benefit]";
7. an escape hatch — "Not now" or "Continue with Free".

Omitting the proof, comparison or escape hatch leaves the user to guess the value or feel trapped. The screen should answer both "what do I get?" and "can I say no?".
