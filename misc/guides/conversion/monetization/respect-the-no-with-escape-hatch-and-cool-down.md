# Give users a clear out and respect dismissal

**Feeds stage:** audit

Every paywall needs a visible "Not now" or "Continue with Free", and declining must not cost the user access to what they were doing. After a dismissal, back off: cap prompts per session, use a cool-down measured in days rather than hours, and track annoyance signals. Do not re-show the same prompt repeatedly.

Preserving the ability to say no keeps trust for a later, better-timed conversion; pressure may win a click now but closes the door on the future one.
