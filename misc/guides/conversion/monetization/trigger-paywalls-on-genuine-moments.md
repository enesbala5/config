# Trigger paywalls on genuine, relevant moments

**Feeds stage:** audit

Tie the prompt to something real the user just did or hit, not an arbitrary timer or the middle of a task:

- **Feature gate** — the user clicked a paid-only feature: explain why it is paid, show what it does, offer a quick unlock and the option to continue without.
- **Usage limit** — the user hit a real limit: show the limit clearly and what upgrading gives, and do not block abruptly.
- **Trial expiration** — warn early (7, 3 and 1 day), say exactly what happens on expiry and summarise the value received.
- **Time-based nudge** — a gentle reminder after sustained free use that highlights unused paid features and is easy to dismiss.

When *not* to show: during onboarding, while the user is in a flow, or repeatedly after dismissal. A prompt that interrupts work teaches users to ignore it.
