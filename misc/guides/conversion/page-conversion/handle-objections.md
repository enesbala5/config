# Handle objections on the page

**Feeds stage:** audit

Address the recurring objections — price/value, "will this work for my
situation?", implementation difficulty, and "what if it doesn't work?" — through
FAQ sections, guarantees, comparison content and process transparency. Include
a comparison against competitors or the status quo when it resolves a real
doubt. An unanswered objection at the decision point costs more than an extra
section of copy.
