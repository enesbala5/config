# Tailor required fields to the form type

**Feeds stage:** audit

Match fields and flow to the form's job. Lead capture: often email only, with
enrichment questions after download. Contact: email/name plus message, phone
optional, response time stated. Demo request: name, email, company required;
phone optional with a preferred-contact choice; a use-case question and a
calendar embed to lift show rate. Quote requests: multi-step with technical
details later. Surveys: progress bar, one question per screen, skip logic.
