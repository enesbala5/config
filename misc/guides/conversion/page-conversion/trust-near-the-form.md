# Reduce form anxiety with trust and objection handling

**Feeds stage:** audit

Near the form, add a privacy statement ("We'll never share your info"), security
badges when collecting sensitive data, a testimonial or social proof, and an
expected response time. Address common objections explicitly — "No spam,
unsubscribe anytime", "We won't share your number", "No credit card required" —
and offer alternatives such as chat or phone on contact forms.
