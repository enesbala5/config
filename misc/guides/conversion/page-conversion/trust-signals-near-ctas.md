# Put trust signals near CTAs and after benefit claims

**Feeds stage:** audit

Place proof where decisions happen: next to CTAs and immediately after benefit
claims. Signals to look for are customer logos (recognisable ones especially),
specific attributed testimonials with photos, case study snippets with real
numbers, review scores and counts, and security badges where relevant. Testable
placements include logos in the hero versus below the fold, testimonials above
the fold versus a dedicated section, and video testimonials instead of text quotes.
