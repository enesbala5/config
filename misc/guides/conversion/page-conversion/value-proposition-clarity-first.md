# Treat value proposition clarity as the highest-impact check

**Feeds stage:** audit

Audit value proposition clarity first: it is named the highest-impact dimension.
Ask whether a visitor can understand what the product is and why they should
care within five seconds, whether the primary benefit is clear, specific and
differentiated, and whether it is written in the customer's language rather
than company jargon. Fix a failing answer here before tuning lower-impact details.
