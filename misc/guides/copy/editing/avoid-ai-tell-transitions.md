# Avoid AI-tell transitional phrases

**Feeds stage:** revise

Cut transitions that signal machine-written copy: "That being said,...", "It's
worth noting that...", "At its core,...", "In today's digital landscape,...",
"When it comes to the realm of...", "This begs the question...", "Let's delve
into...". Do not open a paragraph with "In conclusion", and use "moreover" and
"furthermore" sparingly. Vary the transitions you do keep and don't
over-signpost.

Technical posts add their own openers to the cut list: "In this post we'll dive
into", "Let's get started", "Without further ado", and "that's when it clicked".
Let the section headings carry the transition instead.
