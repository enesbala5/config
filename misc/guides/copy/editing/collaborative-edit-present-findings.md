# In collaborative edits, show findings and propose fixes

**Feeds stage:** revise

When editing with others: run one sweep and present what you found and why;
recommend a specific edit, not just a flag; then request the updated copy so the
author makes the final call. Re-verify earlier sweeps after every round and
repeat until a full sweep finds nothing new. This respects the author's
ownership of the copy while still driving it to clean.
