# Refresh stale content; rewrite only when its angle or audience shifted

**Feeds stage:** revise

Published pages decay — old stats, stale examples, drifted voice. Refresh when
the core message still holds but details are dated (stats over 12 months old,
product changed, competitors updated, declining traffic, AI-search visibility
matters). Rewrite when the topic angle, audience or page structure no longer
fits search intent.

A refresh runs six passes: freshness (dates, stats, examples), accuracy (claims,
links, pricing), voice (current brand tone), SEO (shifted intent, new keywords,
a prominent "Last updated"), proof (newer testimonials and data), structure
(tables, FAQ, scannable formats). Cadence: pricing and product pages quarterly,
high-traffic posts every 6 months, comparison pages every 3–6 months, evergreen
guides annually.
