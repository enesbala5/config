# Quality-gate copy with a 3–5 persona expert panel

**Feeds stage:** revise

For high-stakes copy (launch, pricing, high-traffic landing pages), assemble
3–5 personas relevant to the copy type — for example conversion copywriter, UX
writer, target customer, brand strategist. Each scores 1–10 on their area and
gives specific critiques, not just a number. Revise the lowest-scoring areas
first, re-score, and iterate until every persona is 7+ with a panel average of
8+. Skip this gate for minor edits and low-stakes content; use it optionally for
blog, social and ad copy.
