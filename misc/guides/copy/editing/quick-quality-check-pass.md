# Run a quick quality check on every draft

**Feeds stage:** revise

Before handing off a draft, sweep it for: jargon that could confuse outsiders;
sentences trying to do too much; passive voice; exclamation points (remove
them); and marketing buzzwords without substance. Fix these in place. For a
full line-by-line review, follow with a dedicated editing pass.

At word level, cut weak intensifiers (very, really, extremely) and filler (just,
actually, basically), and unpad phrases ("in order to" → "to"). Replace weak
verbs — utilize → use, leverage → use, facilitate → help, implement → set up —
and collapse nominalizations ("make a decision" → "decide"). At sentence level,
keep one idea per sentence, front-load the point, cap conjunctions at three and
length around 25 words, and vary short with long. At paragraph level, keep one
topic per paragraph in short 2–4 sentence blocks with strong openings and white
space for scannability.
