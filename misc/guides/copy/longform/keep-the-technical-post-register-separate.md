# Keep the technical-post register separate

**Feeds stage:** longform

This voice is a first-person account of a setup that already works on the
author's machine — not general "blog writing", and not the client-marketing
register used in SEO / template-vs-custom posts. Do not blend the two; a
template-style marketing post is the wrong model here.

Calibrate on the author's own published posts: prefer the newer one as the
quality bar and use the older one only for shared structure. Match the rhythm,
not the defects — never copy typos, truncated comments, or stuffed keyword
lists from an older post just because they appear in it.
