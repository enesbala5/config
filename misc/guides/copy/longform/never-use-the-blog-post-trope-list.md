# Never use the blog-post trope list

**Feeds stage:** longform

Cut the register-breaking filler outright: "Excited to share", founder-journey
and LinkedIn cadence, "not X, just Y", "no fluff", "it's easier than you think",
listicle titles ("5 ways to …"), motivational wrap-ups, and the SEO /
template-post marketing voice. Never open on a fake-curiosity hook ("Have you
ever struggled with…").

Do not invent a config and present it as the author's, and keep emoji out of
prose (an emoji inside a real `notify-send` string is fine). Generic AI-tell
transition phrases are a separate editing sweep.
