# Open on the reader's setup

**Feeds stage:** longform

Introduction starts on the situation the reader is already in, then pivots to
the part that is slow or annoying. Opening on a definition or a fake-curiosity
question loses the reader.

Scope out anything with good existing docs, state what the article will not
re-explain, and end with a one-sentence end state ("one keypress flips
polarity" — not "in this article we'll explore"). Add a link to the public
config when the files live there.
