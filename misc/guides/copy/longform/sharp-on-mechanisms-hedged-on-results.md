# Sharp on mechanisms, hedged on results

**Feeds stage:** longform

Be definitive about mechanisms (`lib.mkForce` is important — without it …) and
honest about personal results ("that didn't work for me personally"). Name what
does not update instantly, and do not sell a setup as clean if it needs a
workaround.

A callout is the right place for the caveat: `/tmp` clearing on reboot, apps
that need all instances closed, a hostname already in use. Credibility comes
from naming the messy parts, not from smoothing them over.
