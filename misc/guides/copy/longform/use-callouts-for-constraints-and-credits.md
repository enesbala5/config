# Use callouts for constraints and credits

**Feeds stage:** longform

GitHub/Obsidian callouts (`> [!NOTE]`, `> [!warning]`, `> [!caution]`, `> [!info]`)
get a short title and a real job: inspiration or credit, a hard requirement,
security scope, "keep this running", a distro-specific command, or a personal
trade-off. Do not call out every paragraph.
