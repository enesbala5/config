# Be direct; don't bury the value in qualifications

**Feeds stage:** write

Get to the point and let the value land before the caveats.

- Weak: "Slack lets you share files instantly, from documents to images,
  directly in your conversations"
- Strong: "Need to share a screenshot? Send as many documents, images, and
  audio files as your heart desires."

Don't open with company history or vague statements; lead with the reader's
problem or desired outcome. Burying the point under qualifications makes the
value miss.
