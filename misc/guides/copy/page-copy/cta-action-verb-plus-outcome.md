# Build CTAs from an action verb plus what they get

**Feeds stage:** write

Use the formula [Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier if needed].
Avoid generic labels — Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here, Get Started.
Prefer "Start My Free Trial", "Get the Complete Checklist", "See Pricing for
My Team". "Start Free Trial" beats "Sign Up" because it says what the visitor
receives. Make the CTA obvious, early and repeated — don't let it come after so
much buildup that it's missed, and don't end with a vague "Contact us" in place
of a clear next step.
