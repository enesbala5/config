# Make how-it-works steps numbered, simple and outcome-oriented

**Feeds stage:** write

Each step should be numbered to signal progress, open with a simple verb
("Connect", "Set up", "Get") and say what the reader gets from that step.

Example: 1. Connect your tools (takes 2 minutes); 2. Set your preferences;
3. Get automated reports every Monday.

Three or four steps reduce perceived complexity.
