# Remove hesitation near the CTA

**Feeds stage:** write

Treat every reason to hesitate at the point of action as copy to fix. Near the
CTA, add risk reversers and trust signals — money-back guarantee, free trial,
"no credit card required", "cancel anytime", social proof, privacy assurances —
and a crystal-clear next step. Address objections right there: don't raise a
concern and leave it unanswered, don't let fine print create doubt, and don't
swap a vague "Contact us" for a clear commitment step. Check that the ask
doesn't demand trust the copy hasn't earned.
