# Replace vague claims with specific numbers

**Feeds stage:** write

Choose specific over vague; a concrete figure is credible where a general claim
is forgettable.

- Vague: "Save time on your workflow"
- Specific: "Cut your weekly reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes"
- "Many customers" → "2,847 teams"; "fast results" → "results in 14 days";
  "great support" → "response within 2 hours"

Strike substance-free buzzwords — "streamline", "optimize", "innovative" — and
name what actually changes. Watch for round numbers that feel made up, benefits
without quantification, outcomes without timeframes, and adjectives doing the
work nouns should do. If a claim can't be made specific, cut it — it's filler.
