# Choose testimonials with specific results, not vague praise

**Feeds stage:** write

Favour quotes with concrete outcomes, before/after context and a name, role and
company for credibility — "increased conversions by 32%", "We used to spend
hours...". Reject the empty ones: "Great product!", "Love it!", "Easy to use!".

If a testimonial is vague, go back to the customer with questions about the
specific result, the before-state context and the outcome, then use their words.
Provide a template of what a strong testimonial looks like, but never invent
the numbers yourself.
