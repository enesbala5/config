# Answer the person before making your point

**Feeds stage:** social

Answer what the person actually said first; your own angle comes second. Lead
with a verdict on their question or claim, in their terms, then bring in your
reason for responding.

Counterexample: opening with "As someone who..." or "I built...". The other
person is pushed aside and the reply reads as a pitch. Their question first,
his angle second — explaining their own point back to them is condescending,
not helpful.
