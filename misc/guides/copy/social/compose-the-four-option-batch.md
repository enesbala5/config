# Compose the batch as two options per target with mixed sources

**Feeds stage:** social

A full batch is four options: two for the personal account, two for the brand
account. Across the four, aim for two built from recent work, one topical or
reaction option, and one free slot for whichever signal is stronger that day
(tie-break: his own work). Never four ideas from one commit.

When a target has no fresh work in the window, its build option comes from its
own docs — a mechanism worth explaining or a case-study fact — and the `from:`
line says so.

Pick the change with a real story behind it (a decision, a bug, a trade-off, a
lesson), not the one with the biggest diff. A weak batch of three honest
options beats a fabricated topical reaction; pad with another build-in-public
option rather than inventing the news.
