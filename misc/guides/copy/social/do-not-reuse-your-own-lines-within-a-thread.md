# Do not reuse your own lines within a thread

**Feeds stage:** social

Do not reuse an opener already used in the same thread, and do not repeat his
previous comment back to the other person. Each reply earns its own first line.

A reused opener is the thread-level equivalent of a repeated angle: it reads as
canned and wastes the reply. Read the earlier comments before drafting so the
new line is genuinely new.
