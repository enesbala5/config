# Match the register already used in the thread

**Feeds stage:** social

Before drafting, read the register used in that thread and write in it. The
voice is earnest, slightly formal English with his own grammar shape left
intact: concrete nouns, names, numbers and stack over adjectives, short
sentences, and one real question only when he wants something back. Normal
capitalization, casual joins and comma splices stay; no headers, no bold and no
bullets in a comment.

Do not smooth the grammar into native-speaker polish, and do not add corporate
polish or growth-hacker cadence. Keep blunt verdicts blunt — "99% of the time"
beats "in my experience, many employers request one". Do not soften a claim he
would make flat out.
