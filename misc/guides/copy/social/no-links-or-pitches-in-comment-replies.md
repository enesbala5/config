# Keep links and pitches out of comment replies

**Feeds stage:** social

A comment reply carries no link, no bare domain, no pitch, no feature list and
no CTA. The link lives in the bio; offer to send it instead of pasting it. The
one allowed commercial move is the early-access offer in the house shape, and
even that asks nothing of the reader.

Naming the product in the first line, listing features or dropping the URL
turns the reply into an ad and spends it. Peer and peer-adjacent threads get the
unnamed build line, never the pitch. An offer only works where the person has
the pain the product was built for; on a thread where nobody is job hunting it
is noise.

Counterexample: "We built Coverlttr, it tailors letters and exports to LaTeX,
here's the link" — reads as an ad, not a reply.
