# Read the real thread before drafting a reply

**Feeds stage:** social

Read the actual thread first and never draft for a post you could not read.
Verify it exists and is recent: a months-old post earns no reply, only one that
is hours or days old does.

When material arrives as one paste, separate who said what — the post body, his
own earlier comment and the incoming reply. His own words stay out of the draft;
do not hand them back to him. If a link or screenshot cannot be read, ask for the
text instead of guessing at the content.

Write down the analysis that decides the draft before writing: the question or
claim in their words, what they built and how far they got, what they already
know (explaining their point back reads as condescending), the honest
disagreement or sharper thing they missed, what the target wants, and the one
line only he can write from something he actually ran into. If there is nothing
real to add, say so instead of producing a filler comment.
