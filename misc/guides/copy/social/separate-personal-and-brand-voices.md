# Keep the personal and brand voices separate

**Feeds stage:** social

Two targets, two registers that never blend. The same idea changes shape by
account: on the personal account a product story is about building it ("I spent
two years so I would not have to prep cover letters by hand"); on the brand
account it is a product claim ("the tool produces a draft, you prepare the
letter"). Read the target reference before drafting, and reread its banned list
before the self-check — the two accounts have different bans.

Brand copy on the personal account is the fastest way to make a builder's feed
look like a product feed. Personal options must sound like the person, not a
brand; brand options must not import his personal register.

Counterexample: the same Coverlttr topic written as a brand feature list on the
personal account, or as a chatty first-person build log on the brand account.
Both fail the separation test.
