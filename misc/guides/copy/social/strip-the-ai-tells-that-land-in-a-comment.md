# Strip the AI tells that land in a comment

**Feeds stage:** social

Run a dedicated anti-AI voice check over the reply before handing it over, and
fix everything it flags rather than leaving it in. Take its rules, not its
output format: still one recommended reply and one alternative, never a
draft-audit-final triple. The check owns the full list of tells, so this rule
names only the ones that actually land in a short comment: rule-of-three
padding, "not just X, it's Y", promotional adjectives, signposting, reassurance
kickers, a rhetorical question answered in the next sentence, and hyphenated
pair overuse.

Dashes and bold/bullets are already covered by the format bans. Also ask the
plain stranger test: would a reader take this as an ad?
