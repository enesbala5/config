# Trace every claim to a named source in a short from: line

**Feeds stage:** social

Every option carries one `from:` line naming its source in a few words, and
every concrete claim in the draft traces to that source or to material just
read. An option whose claim cannot be tied to something actually in the
material does not ship.

For a topical option, the item must be real, recent and carry a non-obvious
take of his, not a summary. Its `from:` line names the outlet and the date
exactly as they appeared in the search result so he can check it later. A
paraphrase dressed up as reporting is not a source. If nothing qualifies, ship
a build-in-public option instead and say so in one line.

Example: "from: portfolio 3441294, Incus network activation" or "from: topical,
CV-Library study via The Star, 11 Sep" — short and specific enough to look up.
