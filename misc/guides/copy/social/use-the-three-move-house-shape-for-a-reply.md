# Use the three-move house shape for a reply

**Feeds stage:** social

The recommended comment reply follows three moves, in order:

1. **Settle the dilemma they wrote.** A verdict they can act on, in his flat
   register. Not a compliment and not their post summarized back at them.
2. **The build line, personal and unnamed.** Their pain first, then his own
   reason for building in the same breath. No product name, no feature list,
   no stack.
3. **The offer, when the target allows it.** Give it a reason to stand on (a
   date, a stage), then one sentence that asks nothing of them: if they want
   early access, he will send a link. The link is sent, never pasted.

The alternative reply may take a different angle, but the recommended one
follows this shape. Pre-launch framing goes stale once the product is live:
switch to live wording instead of reusing the old sentence.
