# Prefer always-on reach over launch bursts

**Feeds stage:** plan

Growth comes from penetration — acquiring more buyers, especially light and
non-buyers — not from loyalty. Favour continuous reach that builds availability;
launches fade while availability compounds. Plan for repeated exposure rather than
a single impression: prospects need roughly seven touchpoints before converting,
and familiarity breeds liking, so retargeting, email sequences and consistent
presence compound into preference. Counterexample: loyalty programmes skew to heavy
buyers who would buy anyway, so they flatter retention without adding growth.
