# Ask for the minimum and qualify later

**Feeds stage:** plan

Ask only for information you will actually use. Email alone converts highest; email
plus name enables personalisation at a slight cost; email plus company or role
improves qualification with more friction; multi-field forms are only justified for
high-value offers such as webinars or demos. Every extra field costs roughly 5–10%
of conversion.

Cut any field nobody uses in follow-up, and use progressive profiling: capture the
email now, then enrich or ask for more later. Counterexample: name, email, company,
role, company size and phone on a checklist download.
