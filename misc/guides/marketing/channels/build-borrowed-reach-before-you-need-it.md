# Build borrowed reach before you need it

**Feeds stage:** plan

Borrowed channels — guest posts, podcast interviews, co-marketing, influencer
partnerships, speaking — shortcut being noticed, but they are proactive work
with lead time, not something to arrange in launch week. List the industry
leaders your audience follows, look for audience overlap, pitch win-win
collaborations, and provide value in communities before you pitch. Set up
affiliate or referral incentives so borrowed attention keeps paying off.

Example: TRMNL gifted an e-ink display to a YouTuber with no paid sponsorship;
the review drove 500K+ views and $500K+ in sales, backed by an ongoing affiliate
program. Counterexample: trying to line up podcast interviews and influencer
reviews in the days just before launch — those relationships take weeks to build.
