# Build the community around a shared identity, not the product

**Feeds stage:** plan

Members join because of the product but stay for the people and the identity the
group reinforces. Define in one sentence who members are or aspire to be, then let
channel names, guidelines and rituals express it. Test every decision with: what
identity does this reinforce?

Examples: indie hackers (bootstrapped founders), r/homelab (self-hosting
tinkerers), the Figma community (designers who care about craft).

Counterexample: a "users of X" group where every prompt is about the product. Once
the product stops changing there is no reason to return.
