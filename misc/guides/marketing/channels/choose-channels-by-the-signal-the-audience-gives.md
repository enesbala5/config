# Choose your few channels by the signal the audience gives

**Feeds stage:** plan

Start with one or two channels, selected from what the audience actually lacks
or wants rather than from a generic channel list: an industry short on quality
content → a blog; people who want direct updates → email; an engagement-hungry
audience → a community. On rented platforms, pick the one or two places the
audience is already active.

Counterexample: spreading thin across every social platform just to "be
everywhere" — presence without a reason to exist on that channel.
