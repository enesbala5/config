# Choose the community platform by audience and ownership, not features

**Feeds stage:** plan

Map each platform to where the member already is and what the community must do:

- **Slack** — B2B and professional communities; buyers already live there, but the
  free tier limits history and it can feel like work.
- **Discord** — developer, gaming and creator communities needing real-time chat;
  noisy, hard to search, and there is onboarding friction.
- **Circle** — creator or course communities with clean UX; little organic
  discovery, so you must drive the traffic yourself.
- **Reddit** — high-volume public reach and SEO; you do not own it and moderation
  is hard.
- **Facebook Groups** — consumer brands and older demographics; declining organic
  reach, algorithm-dependent.
- **Discourse forum** — long-form technical discussion and SEO; slower velocity
  and more effort to post.

Decide the trade-off explicitly: owned versus rented, real-time versus long-form,
professional versus hobby context. A B2B SaaS whose buyers are at work during the
day should default to Slack or a forum; Discord only fits if those buyers are
developers or hobbyists in that moment.
