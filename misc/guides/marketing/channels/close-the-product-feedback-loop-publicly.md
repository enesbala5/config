# Announce when community feedback changes the product and credit the members

**Feeds stage:** plan

Close the loop in public: when community feedback drives a change, say so and name
the members who raised it. It proves that influence on the roadmap is real —
the strongest value a community can offer — and gives everyone else a reason to
speak up.

Counterexample: quietly shipping a much-requested feature and letting the
requesters discover it in a changelog.
