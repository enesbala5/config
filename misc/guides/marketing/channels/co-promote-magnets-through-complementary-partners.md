# Co-promote magnets through complementary partners

**Feeds stage:** plan

Partner distribution borrows trust and reach at low cost: cross-promote with
complementary brands, run guest webinars for each other's audiences, include the
magnet in partner newsletters and bundle it into resource collections.

The partner's audience must share the problem but not buy a competing product.
Counterexample: a "partner" whose audience overlaps yours as buyers of the same
category — the co-promotion competes instead of compounding, and both lists learn
about a substitute.
