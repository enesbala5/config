# Reallocate effort to underpriced attention before it burns out

**Feeds stage:** plan

Attention is the only asset. Constantly shift effort to channels where attention is
cheap relative to its value, because "marketers ruin everything" — any working
channel gets piled into and burned out. Examples of the shift: radio → AdWords →
YouTube pre-roll → organic short-form. Diagnostic: where is attention underpriced
right now, and why aren't you there yet? Let data, not fashion, find the
undervalued channel ("moneyball" marketing) — the edge is a channel whose price
has not caught up with the value it delivers.
