# Define one weekly core loop and reward it publicly

**Feeds stage:** plan

Name the single action you want members to take every week — answer a question,
share a work-in-progress, post a win — then make it easy and recognise it
publicly. The loop is the habit the community is built on; without one it becomes
a noticeboard the company posts to.

Counterexample: five channels, a monthly newsletter and no weekly action members
are expected to take.
