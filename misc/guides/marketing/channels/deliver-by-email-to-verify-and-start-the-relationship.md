# Deliver by email to verify the address and start the relationship

**Feeds stage:** plan

Delivery is part of the capture strategy, not an afterthought:

- **Instant download** — immediate gratification, but no email verification and no
  first email.
- **Email delivery** — verifies the address and starts the relationship, at the cost
  of a slight delay.
- **Thank-you page plus email** — instant access and the email copy; best of both at
  slightly more complexity.
- **Drip delivery** — builds a habit across touchpoints, but only suits courses or
  series.

Counterexample: an instant PDF download when the goal is list growth — the address
is unverified and the first touch is missing.
