# Double down where you have a real edge

**Feeds stage:** plan

Stay inside your circle of competence: back the channels where you have genuine
expertise or competitive advantage, and learn or hire help before entering the
rest. Not chasing every channel is a decision, not a limitation. Diagnostic: for
each candidate channel, what do you do better than the alternatives, and what
would it cost to become competent? Counterexample: copying a competitor into a
channel they own and you do not.
