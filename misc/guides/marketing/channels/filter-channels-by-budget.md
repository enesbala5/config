# Treat budget as a hard filter on channel choice

**Feeds stage:** plan

Budget decides which tactics are available at all, so filter before ranking:

- Free — content & SEO, community, social, comment marketing.
- Low — targeted ads, sponsorships, free tools.
- Medium — events, partnerships, PR.
- High — acquisitions, conferences, brand campaigns.

Counterexample: recommending conferences or brand campaigns to a bootstrapped
team with $500/month. The constraint rules those out before their merits are
considered; an unaffordable tactic is not a "stretch goal", it is a wrong answer.
