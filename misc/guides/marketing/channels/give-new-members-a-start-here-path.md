# Give new members a start-here path

**Feeds stage:** plan

Convert joiners into posters with an explicit first-week path: a pinned welcome
post, an #introduce-yourself channel, a DM or email from the community manager,
and one clear first action. The goal is a first post within days, not a tour of
every channel.

Counterexample: a welcome email with ten links and no next step; members lurk, then
churn, and the intro thread is the only thing they ever write.
