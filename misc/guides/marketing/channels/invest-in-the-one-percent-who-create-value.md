# Invest in the 1% of members who create most of the value

**Feeds stage:** plan

Roughly 1% of members generate 90% of a community's value. Find them by who
answers questions, welcomes newcomers and creates content, then give them
recognition, moderator roles, early access or direct product input. Their
continued participation is the community's supply.

Counterexample: spending all community time converting lurkers while the people
already carrying the room get nothing.
