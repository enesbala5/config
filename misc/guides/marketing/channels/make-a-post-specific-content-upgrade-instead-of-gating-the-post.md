# Make a post-specific content upgrade instead of gating the post

**Feeds stage:** plan

When a blog post performs, attach a bonus that matches what the reader is already
reading instead of gating the post. Post-specific content upgrades convert 2–5x
better than generic sidebar CTAs because relevance and intent are higher, and
keeping the post ungated preserves its search traffic.

Example: a post on email subject lines becomes "50 subject-line templates", a
one-page cheat sheet or an annotated swipe file, offered inline or at the end.
Counterexample: putting the whole article behind a form and losing both the search
traffic and the reader's context.
