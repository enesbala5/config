# Make value flow to members before it flows to the business

**Feeds stage:** plan

Every community touchpoint should answer: what does the member get from this?
Name it from a short list — exclusive knowledge or early access, peer connections
they cannot get elsewhere, recognition in a group they respect, influence on the
roadmap, career visibility.

If a post, event or channel only serves the company, cut it or rebalance it so the
member gets something first.

Counterexample: a "community" that is really a support queue plus an announcement
feed.
