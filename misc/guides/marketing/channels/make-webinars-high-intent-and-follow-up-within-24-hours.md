# Make webinars high-intent and follow up within 24 hours

**Feeds stage:** plan

Webinars trade volume for quality: expect 20–40% registration conversion, 30–50%
attendance of registrants, low-to-medium lead volume and high lead quality. They
work when they teach something genuinely useful; a thinly disguised sales pitch
damages trust.

Send confirmation plus reminders the day before and an hour before, record for
replay, and follow up within 24 hours with the replay, a summary and a CTA.
Counterexample: a webinar that is a product demo in disguise, followed by silence
after the session.
