# Map the people who can tell the brand story

**Feeds stage:** plan

Plan distribution around people, not just the brand account. For each founder,
employee, expert, creator, or customer who could participate, write down the
role they can credibly play, where they can create regularly, and whose account
the content should run on. A makeup artist without a large following can lend
credibility on the brand feed; a creator with an audience can bring reach on
their own feed. A repeatable setting, such as a fitness studio for coaches,
makes regular contributions easier.

Start with customers and newsletter subscribers before searching cold. Creator
discovery tools can help find public creator profiles associated with an
existing email list; use only data you're allowed to use and check each person's
fit before reaching out.
