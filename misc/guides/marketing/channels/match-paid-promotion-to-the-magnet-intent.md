# Match paid promotion to the magnet's intent

**Feeds stage:** plan

Use the platform whose native intent matches the magnet:

- Facebook/Instagram lead ads for top-funnel magnets.
- Google Ads for high-intent templates and tools.
- LinkedIn for B2B magnets.
- Retargeting for people who already visited the blog.

Counterexample: pushing a beginner checklist to cold LinkedIn traffic at $25–75 per
lead when the same asset would earn cheaper volume on Meta; or using broad cold
social targeting for a decision-stage template. Paid CPL varies by an order of
magnitude across channels, so the channel choice is part of the offer's economics.
