# Match the tactic to the goal's time horizon

**Feeds stage:** plan

Set expectations from the timeline the requester actually cares about:

- Quick wins — ads, email, social posts.
- Medium-term — content, SEO, community.
- Long-term — brand, thought leadership, platform effects.

When the goal is explicitly "leads fast", the honest answer is a paid or
outbound channel — high-intent search, B2B targeting, direct outreach — not SEO.
Conversely, authority and thought leadership are a long-term play: do not sell
them as a way to get leads this month.
