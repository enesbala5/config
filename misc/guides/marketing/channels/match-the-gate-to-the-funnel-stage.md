# Match the gate to the funnel stage

**Feeds stage:** plan

Gating is a reach-versus-capture trade-off, so pick the gate from the content's job:

- **Full gate** — high-value, bottom-funnel content: maximum capture, lower reach.
- **Partial gate** — preview plus full version: balances reach and capture.
- **Ungated with optional email** — top-funnel education: maximum reach, lower
  capture.
- **Content upgrade** — a bonus attached to a blog post: contextual and high-intent.

Counterexample: fully gating an awareness-stage guide and wondering why it never
travels. The gate should tighten as the content gets closer to a purchase decision.
