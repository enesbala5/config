# Onboard early users one at a time to compound word of mouth

**Feeds stage:** plan

At launch, personal onboarding is a growth mechanism, not a support cost. Recruit
and onboard early users individually — a live demo or hands-on setup session per
new user — instead of leaving them to self-serve. The high-touch relationship
creates exclusivity and word of mouth, surfaces friction you can fix before
scale, and produces onboarding material you can reuse later.

Example: Superhuman built demand through an invite-only waitlist plus a 30-minute
live demo for every new user; years later its original onboarding material still
drove engagement.

Counterexample: opening signups to everyone at once with a generic welcome email,
then wondering why early users never activate or refer anyone.
