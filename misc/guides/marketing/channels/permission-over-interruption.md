# Earn permission instead of buying interruption

**Feeds stage:** plan

Interruption advertising steals attention and grows less effective; earn permission
with messages that are anticipated, personal and relevant to people who opted in.
Concrete move: prefer an owned opted-in list or community over rented reach, and
gate a genuinely useful asset behind opt-in so the first touch is invited rather
than bought. This is an ethical limit as well as a channel choice: marketing is
something you do for people, not to them.
