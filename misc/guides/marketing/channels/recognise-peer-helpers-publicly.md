# Recognise peer helpers so support deflection feels valued

**Feeds stage:** plan

Publicly recognise members who answer others — "Community Expert" badges,
leaderboards or shoutouts. Community-led support only works when peer answers are
treated as valuable, not as a way for the company to dodge responsibility: keep
staff present so questions do not sit unanswered.

Counterexample: routing all support into the community, thanking nobody, and
letting the same three volunteers burn out.
