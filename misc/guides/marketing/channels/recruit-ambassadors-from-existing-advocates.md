# Recruit ambassadors from people already recommending you

**Feeds stage:** plan

Look for people who already recommend you unprompted — in reviews, social
mentions and community posts — before inventing an ambassador programme. Ask each
one personally, one to one, and explain why you chose them specifically; a generic
form produces few serious takers.

Offer benefits that matter beyond "early access to features": exclusive access,
swag, revenue share, public recognition or product input. Then give them the tools
— referral links, shareable assets, key talking points, a private channel — and
track referral traffic, signups and engagement to see what works.

Counterexample: a mass "become an ambassador" signup page followed by a generic
welcome kit nobody uses.
