# Route every channel back to owned channels

**Feeds stage:** plan

Structure launch marketing around three channel types and make owned the hub:
owned channels (email list, blog, community, website) compound, face no algorithm
changes or pay-to-play, and give a direct relationship; rented channels (social,
marketplaces, app stores) give speed, not stability; borrowed channels (guest
content, collaborations, influencers) give instant credibility. Every tactic in
a rented or borrowed channel should funnel attention into an owned relationship.

Example: Notion turned viral Twitter, YouTube and Reddit posts into signups,
then ran targeted email onboarding. Counterexample: treating follower counts as
the goal, or relying on a rented platform as the only strategy.
