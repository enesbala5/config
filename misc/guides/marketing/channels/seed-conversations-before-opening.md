# Seed conversations before you open the community

**Feeds stage:** plan

Pre-populate channels with 5–10 posts that model the behaviour you want: open
questions, member wins, useful resources. A new member who lands in an empty room
leaves; a room with three live threads teaches the expected behaviour without a
rulebook.

Counterexample: opening day with only a #welcome post and waiting for members to
start the first conversation.
