# Split effort between proven channels and small experiments

**Feeds stage:** plan

Do not bet everything on one campaign, and do not abandon what already works for
shiny new channels. Use a barbell: most of the budget (roughly 80%) in proven
channels, a small slice (roughly 20%) in high-upside experiments, and avoid the
mediocre middle of moderate-risk, moderate-reward bets. Plan for the scenario in
which the primary channel underperforms.
