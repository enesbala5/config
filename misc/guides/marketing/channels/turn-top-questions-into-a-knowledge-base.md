# Turn the top community questions into a searchable knowledge base

**Feeds stage:** plan

Mine recurring questions from the community and write each answer once in a
searchable knowledge base. This deflects repeat tickets, keeps peer answers
findable instead of buried in chat, and gives new members a reason the community
is worth joining.

Counterexample: answering the same onboarding question in DMs every week while the
answer never becomes searchable.
