# Write community guidelines that describe the vibe, not just the rules

**Feeds stage:** plan

State the hard rules (no spam, harassment or piracy) and then the aspirational
part: what great participation looks like here. Match the tone to the audience and
let channel names carry the identity.

Example: guidelines for indie game developers sound plainspoken; they ask members
to share work-in-progress freely, give constructive feedback and lift other devs
up, with channels like #devlog, #playtest-requests and #publishing-tips.

Counterexample: a legalistic rules page that only says what not to do, leaving
members to guess what the community is for.
