# Benchmark conversion by format and traffic source, not one number

**Feeds stage:** verify

Judge a magnet against the right reference class.

Landing-page conversion by format: checklist 30–50%, cheat sheet 25–40%, template
25–45%, quiz 30–50%, ebook 20–35%, mini-course 15–30%, webinar registration 20–40%,
free trial 5–15%.

By traffic source: dedicated page 20–40% organic and 10–25% paid; blog content
upgrade 3–8% of post readers; social link 10–20%; exit-intent popup 2–5%;
sidebar/banner 0.5–2%.

A 4% landing page is plausible on cold paid traffic and poor on warm organic, so ask
for the source before calling the offer weak. Treat these as priors, not targets:
results depend on audience, niche, traffic and offer quality, so build your own
baseline from day one.
