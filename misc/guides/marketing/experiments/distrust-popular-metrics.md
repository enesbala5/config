# Stress-test popular metrics and rules of thumb

**Feeds stage:** verify

Treat widely quoted benchmarks with suspicion: the 60:40 brand/activation rule is
built on unsound awards data, and attention metrics have been dismissed as nonsense.
The opposite risk is measurement obsession — chasing perfect attribution over-invests
in what is measurable and under-invests in what matters. Ask what the data actually
shows across categories and time, not what this quarter's anecdote says.
