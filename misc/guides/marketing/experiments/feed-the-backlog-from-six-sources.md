# Feed the hypothesis backlog from six evidence sources

**Feeds stage:** verify

Do not generate hypotheses from opinion. Pull them from:

- **Analytics** — drop-off points, low-converting pages, weak segments;
- **Customer research** — pain points, confusion, unmet expectations;
- **Competitors** — features, messaging or UX patterns they use and you do not;
- **Support tickets** — recurring questions and complaints about conversion
  flows;
- **Heatmaps and recordings** — hesitation, rage clicks, abandonment;
- **Past experiments** — especially significant losers, which often reveal the
  next angle.

A backlog fed by one source converges on one kind of idea. Mixing sources is what
keeps the queue from becoming a list of button-colour tweaks.
