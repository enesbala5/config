# Judge a magnet downstream, by source

**Feeds stage:** verify

Downloads measure the offer, not the lead. Judge by downstream signals: first three
emails opened at 40%+, clicks to content or product pages, a return visit within 30
days, a match to the ICP on role, company size and industry, and progression to
trial, demo or purchase within 90 days.

Bad signs: unsubscribing within the first three emails, never opening beyond the
delivery email, disposable addresses, off-ICP buyers, and downloads with no product
interest. Track time to conversion by magnet source so a slow, high-quality source
is not killed for losing a race it was never in.
