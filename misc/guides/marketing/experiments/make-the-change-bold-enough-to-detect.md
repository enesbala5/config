# Make the change bold enough to detect

**Feeds stage:** verify

A test of too small a change is underpowered by design: the variant cannot
plausibly produce the lift you need, so a null result teaches nothing.

Before shipping, ask whether this change could move the primary metric by the
minimum effect you care about. On a page converting at 3%, a button-colour tweak
is unlikely to; a different message angle, offer, layout, CTA placement or
amount of social proof can.

The change must also stay true to the hypothesis. Boldness is not licence to
redesign everything — pick the one lever the observation points to and move it
far enough that the test can see the difference.
