# Pick one North Star metric and align everything to it

**Feeds stage:** verify

Choose the single metric that best captures the value customers actually receive —
active users, completed projects, revenue per customer — and use it to judge
effort. Focus creates alignment: when every experiment must move the same number,
trade-offs become decidable. Counterexample: optimising clicks or impressions
because they are easy to count while value delivery goes unmeasured.
