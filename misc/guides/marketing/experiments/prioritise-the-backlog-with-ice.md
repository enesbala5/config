# Prioritise the hypothesis backlog with ICE and re-score it monthly

**Feeds stage:** verify

Score each hypothesis 1–10 on Impact (how much it would move the primary metric
if true), Confidence (how much the evidence — not gut — supports it), and Ease
(how fast and cheap it is to ship and measure). Run the highest average first.

Re-score monthly as context changes: a test that was expensive before the new
analytics dashboard may now be cheap, and confidence decays when the supporting
data ages.

Counterexample: running the easiest test in the backlog because it is easy,
while the high-impact, medium-confidence hypothesis starves. Ease is one third of
the score, not the tie-breaker.
