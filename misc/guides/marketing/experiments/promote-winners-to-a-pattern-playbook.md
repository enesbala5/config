# Promote winners to a playbook of reusable patterns

**Feeds stage:** verify

Shipping the variant is the smallest part of a win. Record the hypothesis, sample
size, result with confidence interval, guardrail outcomes, segment deltas, why it
worked or failed, the reusable pattern, and where else that pattern might apply.

The pattern is the asset: "social proof near the pricing CTA raises plan
selection" can be tried on the signup page; "the blue button won" cannot.

Audit the playbook quarterly: which patterns have actually been applied broadly,
which winning patterns are still unscaled, and which funnel areas are
under-tested. Counterexample: shipping a winning headline and forgetting the
reason it won.
