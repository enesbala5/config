# Run tests in whole weeks and cap them at 4–8 weeks

**Feeds stage:** verify

Even with enough sample, duration needs a floor and a ceiling. Run at least one
full week to capture day-of-week variation, two business cycles for B2B
(weekday versus weekend behaviour), and through paydays for e-commerce.

Estimate days as: sample per variant × number of variants ÷ (daily traffic ×
share exposed). If the answer is 120 days, the test is not viable as designed —
change the question rather than waiting a season.

Cap tests at roughly 4–8 weeks. Beyond that, novelty effects wear off, external
factors intervene, and the opportunity cost of other tests dominates. Keep
exposure balanced across times of day and week, and show returning users the
same variant.
