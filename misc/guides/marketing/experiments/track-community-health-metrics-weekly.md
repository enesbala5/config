# Track five community health metrics weekly

**Feeds stage:** verify

Review these together once a week:

- **DAU/MAU** — stickiness; above 20% is healthy for most communities.
- **New member post rate** — share of new members who post within 7 days of
  joining.
- **Thread reply rate** — share of posts that get at least one reply.
- **Lurker ratio** — members who joined but have not posted in 30+ days.
- **Non-staff content share** — share of posts not written by the company team.

Read them as a set. A healthy DAU/MAU with a falling new-member post rate means
the core is fine and the front door is broken; a rising member count with a
falling non-staff share means the community is becoming a broadcast channel.
