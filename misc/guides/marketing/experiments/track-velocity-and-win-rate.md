# Track experiment velocity and win rate as leading indicators

**Feeds stage:** verify

Programme health shows up before revenue does. Track five numbers:

- experiments launched per month — 4–8 for most teams;
- win rate — 20–30% is common for a mature programme;
- average test duration — 2–4 weeks;
- backlog depth — 20+ hypotheses queued;
- cumulative lift — the compounded gain from all winners.

A sustained win rate well above 30% is not a triumph; it usually means the
hypotheses are too safe. A collapsing launch rate means the programme has become
a queue of unstarted ideas. Counterexample: judging the programme only by the
last big win.
