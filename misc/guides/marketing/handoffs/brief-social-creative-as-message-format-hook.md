# Brief social creative as message × format × hook

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Hand off a small matrix: what the audience should take away, which formats
the team can actually produce, and what opening would earn attention without
misrepresenting the content. Pull messages from core differentiators, real
customer stories, and timely questions. Only include formats you have the
people, footage, or design time to make. Pick viable combinations rather than
producing every cell.

Example: for a gym that helps people new to town find community, brief a
coach-led walkthrough that opens on the first group session, or a carousel that
opens with a newcomer's question. The format and hook change; the message
doesn't.
