# Budget community management as a real weekly role

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Early community-building is deliberately unscalable: reply to every post, welcome
every member by name, host a weekly call. Before committing to the channel, decide
who owns it and how many hours a week they have; the strategy is not viable
without that capacity.

Counterexample: launching a community and assigning it to whoever has spare time.
Unanswered posts teach members the room is dead, and the channel quietly fails.
