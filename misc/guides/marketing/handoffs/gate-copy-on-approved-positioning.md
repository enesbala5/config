# Gate copy production on approved positioning and angle

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Do not write any copy until positioning and the campaign angle are approved. The
gate is a document, not a feeling: an approved positioning brief and a locked tone
profile must exist and be signed off before a landing page, email or ad is briefed.

Positioning is where campaign direction is decided; copy is where it is executed.
Writing copy first inverts that order and turns review into an argument about
strategy that should already be settled. If the positioning is still moving, the
correct next step is to finish positioning, not to draft a hero section.
