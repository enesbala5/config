# Gate the campaign on cross-deliverable consistency

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Review the campaign as one set, not piece by piece. Per-piece polish hides the
failures that only appear between deliverables:

- ad claims match the landing page claims exactly — not approximately;
- each email subject matches its body (no bait-and-switch);
- every piece sounds like the same author;
- no copy is duplicated verbatim across platforms;
- hero and above-fold copy pass the five-second test: who it is for, what it does,
  why act now.

A landing page can pass review alone and still contradict the ad that sends traffic
to it. Run the consistency gate before anything goes live, and treat a mismatch as
a blocker rather than a note.
