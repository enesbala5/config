# Hand off a magnet brief, not a finished asset

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Strategy owns the magnet's job, audience, stage and success metric; production owns
the artifact. The handoff should specify the format and topic, the target buyer
stage, why that format fits this audience, the estimated creation effort, the
capture and gating plan, the distribution plan, and the KPIs plus the first test.

Then copy writes the content, design produces the asset and CRO optimises the
landing page. Counterexample: a strategy session that drafts the ebook itself and
leaves the capture, distribution and measurement decisions unmade.
