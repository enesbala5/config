# Hand research off to the right downstream specialist

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Research produces evidence, not finished downstream work. Route it onward by
intent: copy informed by research → copy (write); page optimisation from VOC → cro;
competitor comparison page → competitors; churn strategy → churn-prevention;
paid ads → ads; pain/trigger-based outreach → cold-email; ICP for outbound →
prospecting; content topics → content-strategy; full marketing plan →
marketing-plan.
