# Lock the tone profile before production and hand voice capture off

**Feeds stage:** handoff

Positioning includes tone, and tone must be fixed before production starts, not
negotiated per piece. Capture it as a durable profile — vocabulary, sentence
rhythm, what the brand would and would not say — so every writer and channel works
from the same source.

The strategy layer sets the tone direction; durable voice capture belongs to the
voice skill. Hand it off rather than re-deriving brand voice in each campaign
brief. Re-deciding tone per deliverable is how a campaign ends up sounding like
several different authors.
