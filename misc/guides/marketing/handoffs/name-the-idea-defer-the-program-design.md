# Name the idea, defer the program design

**Feeds stage:** handoff

The ideas catalog names tactics; it does not design them. When a request asks how
to structure a specific program — a referral program, a free tool, an email
sequence — hand off to the skill that owns that design and mention the idea only
as context.

Counterexample: attempting full referral-loop and incentive design from the
ideas layer instead of deferring to the referrals skill; the ideas layer can say
"referrals fit your stage", but not how the loop should work.

The same split applies to launches: a launch plan decides the phase, the channel
mix and which touchpoints fire on launch day. The announcement email copy, the
landing-page conversion work and the onboarding sequence belong to the skills
that own them — include them in the plan as placeholders, not as drafts.
