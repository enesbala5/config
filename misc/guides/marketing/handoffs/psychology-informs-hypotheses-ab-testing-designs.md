# Psychology informs the hypothesis; A/B testing designs the experiment

**Feeds stage:** handoff

A mental model tells you what might influence behaviour — it is not an experiment
design. When the request is to compare two principles or two variants, hand the
design, sample and stopping rule to A/B testing and use psychology only to state
the hypothesis. Counterexample: attempting a full significance calculation from a
persuasion framework, or presenting a "psychology says" verdict without a test.
