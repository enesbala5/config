# Clear the pre-launch gate before you announce

**Feeds stage:** plan

Do not announce until the launch infrastructure exists. The pre-launch gate:
landing page with a clear value proposition; email capture or waitlist; an
early-access list; established owned channels; optimised rented profiles;
identified borrowed opportunities; launch assets (screenshots, demo video,
GIFs); a ready onboarding flow; and analytics/tracking in place.

Missing tracking means you cannot learn from launch day; a missing onboarding
flow means the traffic you win cannot activate. Counterexample: driving a spike
to a page with no email capture — the attention is lost the moment the spike ends.
