# Give every deliverable one job in the campaign arc

**Feeds stage:** plan

Before briefing a piece, name the single job it does in the campaign: announce,
educate, agitate, prove, create urgency, or close. A piece with two jobs usually
does neither, and a campaign with no assigned jobs repeats the same message on every
channel.

A launch email sequence is the clearest example: each email gets one purpose and the
series runs problem → education → agitation → solution → proof → urgency → final
call to action. The constraint is what creates progression. Counterexample: one
email that announces the product, explains the category, and asks for a demo —
the reader has no reason to act at any point because nothing was built toward.
