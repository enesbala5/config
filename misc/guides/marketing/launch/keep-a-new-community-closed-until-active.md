# Keep a new community closed until baseline activity exists

**Feeds stage:** plan

Recruit 20–50 founding members by hand — DM your most engaged users, beta testers
or fans — and do not open publicly until there is baseline activity. A public
opening into an empty room sets the norm that nobody posts, and the first cohort
never comes back.

Counterexample: announcing a public Slack on launch day and watching the first few
hundred joiners find zero conversations.
