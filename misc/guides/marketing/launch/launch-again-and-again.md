# Launch again and again instead of relying on one moment

**Feeds stage:** plan

Treat every new feature, improvement and update as an opportunity to capture
attention: get the product into users' hands early, learn from real feedback,
and make a splash at each stage so momentum compounds. Space out releases rather
than shipping everything at once, and reuse tactics that resonated before. Even
a small changelog entry signals active development, building retention and
word-of-mouth because customers believe you will be around.

Counterexample: a single launch event followed by silence until the next big
release.
