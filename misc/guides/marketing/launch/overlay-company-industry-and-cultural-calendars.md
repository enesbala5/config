# Overlay company, industry, and cultural calendars

**Feeds stage:** plan

Before locking a launch window or briefing creators, put company milestones,
industry events, and audience-relevant cultural moments on the same calendar.
Mark where an outside event gives your story a timely angle and where it is
likely to crowd out an unrelated announcement. Assign a person to cover the
moments you choose instead of trying to join every trend.

Example: an app team can show how its interface works on newly announced
hardware while people are looking for demos. An unrelated launch aimed at
shoppers might be better off avoiding Black Friday.
