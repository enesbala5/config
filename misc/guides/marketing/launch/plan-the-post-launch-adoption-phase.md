# Plan the post-launch adoption phase, not just the announcement

**Feeds stage:** plan

The announcement going live is the start of the work, not the end. Schedule the
adoption and retention phase alongside the launch: an automated onboarding email
sequence, a mention in the regular roundup email to catch people who missed it,
comparison pages against alternatives, updated site sections for the new product
or feature, and an interactive demo so visitors can explore before signing up.
Reinforce the launch at every touchpoint — momentum is easier to carry than to
rebuild from scratch.
