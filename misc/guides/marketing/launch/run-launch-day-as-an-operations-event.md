# Run launch day as an operations event, not a publish action

**Feeds stage:** plan

Launch day is several touchpoints firing at once plus a team standing behind
them. Schedule the announcement email, blog post, social posts, any platform
listing, an in-app announcement for existing users, and a site banner. Staff
people to answer comments and questions in real time, monitor for product
issues and feedback, and route every channel's traffic to a signup or waitlist
you control.

Counterexample: publishing the blog post and going back to work — comments sit
unanswered, a broken signup form goes unnoticed for hours, and the day's
attention arrives with nothing to catch it.
