# Scale announcement effort to the size of the update

**Feeds stage:** plan

Not every release deserves a full campaign. Use a three-level matrix:

- Major — new feature or product overhaul: full multi-channel campaign (blog post, email campaign, in-app messages, social).
- Medium — integration or UI enhancement: targeted email to relevant segments plus an in-app banner, without full fanfare.
- Minor — bug fix or small tweak: changelog and release notes only; signal progress without dominating marketing.

Counterexample: treating a minor bug fix like a product launch — it burns
attention and trains your audience to ignore announcements.
