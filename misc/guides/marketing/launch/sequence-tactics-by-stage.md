# Sequence tactics by stage, not all at once

**Feeds stage:** plan

Choose tactics from the shortlist for the current stage:

- Pre-launch — waitlist referrals, early-access pricing, Product Hunt prep.
- Early — content & SEO, community, founder-led sales.
- Growth — paid acquisition, partnerships, events.
- Scale — brand campaigns, international expansion, media acquisitions.

A tactic whose prerequisites the stage does not yet have is a poor recommendation
however good it looks in the abstract. Map the request to a stage before picking
anything from the catalog.
