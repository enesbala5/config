# Treat a platform launch as a prepared, all-day event

**Feeds stage:** plan

A Product Hunt–style launch (Product Hunt, BetaList, Hacker News) reaches early
adopters but is not magic: it needs 2–4 weeks of preparation and creates a
short-lived traffic spike. Preparation is the platform-specific job: build
relationships with influential supporters and communities, provide value before
pitching, study successful launches, and optimise the listing. Follow-up is the
second job: reply to everyone who engaged and convert the spike into owned
relationships. The day itself is an operations event — staffing, real-time
responses and routing traffic to owned capture are the same regardless of
platform — so do not treat the listing as the whole launch.

Examples: SavvyCal pre-built influencer relationships, optimised its landing
page and onboarding, and answered every comment, reaching #2 Product of the
Month; Reform studied past launches, engaged communities value-first, and
reached #1 Product of the Day. Counterexample: expecting a listing alone to
produce durable growth with no preparation or follow-up.
