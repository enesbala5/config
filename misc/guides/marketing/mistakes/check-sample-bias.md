# Check who your sources over-represent before concluding 'all customers'

**Feeds stage:** verify

Every source skews: online reviewers are power users and strong opinions,
support tickets are problems not value, Reddit is technical and skeptical,
survey multiple-choice answers are artefacts of the options offered, job
postings are aspirational. Name the bias attached to each source and discount
accordingly; conclusions about the whole market drawn from one skewed channel
are unsupported.
