# Curate ideas by fit; do not dump the catalog

**Feeds stage:** verify

Suggest the 3–5 ideas most relevant to the stated context, each with a reason it
fits. When a large list is explicitly requested, still organise it by category or
by effort/impact and vary across categories so the set is well-rounded.

Counterexample: pasting the full list of 139 ideas — or twenty ideas all from
content & SEO — in response to "give me marketing ideas". Volume without
selection is not a recommendation; it hands the prioritisation back to the
person who asked you to do it.
