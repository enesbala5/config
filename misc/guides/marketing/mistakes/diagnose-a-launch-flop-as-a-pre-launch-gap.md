# Diagnose a launch flop as a pre-launch gap first

**Feeds stage:** verify

When a launch underperforms, look before launch day before blaming the
announcement. The usual causes are insufficient audience building before launch,
the wrong channels, weak value-proposition messaging, poor launch execution, or
targeting the wrong audience. Recovery: find which channels actually produced
the signups you did get and double down there, try a new distribution channel,
iterate the messaging, and use early users for testimonials.

Counterexample: responding to a low signup count by rewriting the announcement
when the real gap was that no audience was built before launch.
