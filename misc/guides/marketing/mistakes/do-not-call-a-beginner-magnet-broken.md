# Do not call a beginner magnet broken when it is doing its stage's job

**Feeds stage:** verify

A checklist that draws beginners and produces few trials is not necessarily broken;
it may be an awareness-stage asset working as designed. Diagnose stage mismatch
before rewriting it.

If the goal was pipeline and the magnet attracts people who are not ready to buy,
either accept a longer nurture path or add a consideration- or decision-stage magnet
such as a template, comparison sheet or ROI calculator. Counterexample: scrapping a
high-volume awareness magnet instead of checking whether the follow-up sequence and
the offer match the stage of the leads it produces.
