# Do not mix formats in one lead magnet

**Feeds stage:** verify

A magnet should have one clear format that works on mobile and needs no special
software. Combining an ebook, a video and a spreadsheet multiplies production effort
and gives the reader three things to start instead of one.

Counterexample: a "toolkit" that is a PDF plus a video series plus a Notion board,
where none is finished well and the download does not say what to do first. If the
ideas genuinely need different formats, split them into separate magnets rather than
shipping a bundle that is weaker than its parts.
