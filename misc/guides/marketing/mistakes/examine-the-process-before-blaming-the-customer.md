# Examine the process before blaming the customer

**Feeds stage:** verify

When people do not convert, look at the situation before judging the person. The
default explanation — "they weren't serious" — is the fundamental attribution
error: we credit character where a broken checkout, a wrong audience or a
confusing message is the real cause. Diagnostic: list what in your process could
have blocked the action before you label the visitor unqualified.
