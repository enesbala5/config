# Separate the model from the customer

**Feeds stage:** verify

A persona, dashboard or mental model is a useful simplification, not the customer
itself. Map is not territory: analytics show traces of behaviour, not the
experience that produced them, and a persona is simpler than any real buyer.
Before acting on a model, ask: have you tested this with real customers, and what
would the data look like if the model were wrong? Stay in touch with actual users.
