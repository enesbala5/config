# Treat company-dominated posts and unanswered questions as red flags

**Feeds stage:** verify

Watch for these signals and diagnose rather than adding activity:

- most posts are written by the company team — members see no reason to
  contribute;
- questions sit unanswered for more than 24 hours — nobody is staffing the room;
- the same five people produce 80%+ of engagement — a clique has formed;
- new members stop posting after their intro message — the first-week path fails.

Each points to a different failure, so the fix is not "post more".

Counterexample: celebrating member count while the feed is all staff
announcements.
