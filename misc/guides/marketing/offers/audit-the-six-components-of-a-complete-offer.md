# Audit the six components of a complete offer

**Feeds stage:** plan

A complete offer has six parts: core deliverable, bonus stack, guarantee,
scarcity/urgency, name, and price + payment structure. Skip one and conversion
suffers; most weak offers are missing bonuses, a guarantee, or honest scarcity,
not a better sales page. When an offer stalls, audit all six and fix the missing
or weak component rather than rewriting the copy. Counterexample: a service
described only by its features, with no name, no guarantee and no reason to act
now.
