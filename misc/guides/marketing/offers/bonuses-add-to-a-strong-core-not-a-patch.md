# Add bonuses to a strong core, never as a patch

**Feeds stage:** plan

Bonuses should be additive to a complete core, not repairs for an incomplete
one. If the bonus is what makes the offer worth buying, you have a weak core —
strengthen the core first, then layer bonuses to close objections. Counterexample:
a sales-page-writing service whose "bonus" is free lifetime edits; the bonus
implies the paid page is incomplete and leaves buyers wondering why they would
buy without it.
