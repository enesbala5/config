# Choose between a static magnet and an interactive tool

**Feeds stage:** plan

Static magnets — checklists, templates, ebooks — ship in hours to days, need little
maintenance and suit awareness or consideration education; lead quality varies.
Interactive tools — calculators, graders, quizzes — take weeks to months but produce
higher engagement and shareability, segment leads by what they input, can rank for
"X calculator" searches and typically yield higher-quality leads.

Choose the static magnet when speed matters. Choose the tool when you can invest the
build time and users have repeatable inputs that produce a meaningful output.
Counterexample: building a calculator before proving anyone wants the underlying
answer.
