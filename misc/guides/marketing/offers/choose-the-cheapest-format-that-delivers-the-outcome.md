# Choose the cheapest format that delivers the outcome

**Feeds stage:** plan

Match the format to effort and audience patience, not ambition. Rough effort ladder:
checklist 1–2 hours; cheat sheet 2–4; template 2–8; swipe file 4–8; quiz or email
mini-course 1–2 weeks; ebook 1–3 weeks; video course longer still. For a busy
audience, a template or checklist usually beats an ebook on utility and time-to-ship.

Example: for agencies, a client-onboarding checklist or capacity-planning template
over a 40-page guide. Counterexample: spending three weeks on an ebook for a problem
a one-page checklist solves.
