# Define the scope explicitly in writing

**Feeds stage:** plan

State what is in, what is out, and what is optional before the sale. Buyers buy
clarity, and ambiguity erodes perceived likelihood; in service work an undefined
scope is the classic offer-killer through creep. Counterexample: "we'll help you
grow" with no timeline or boundaries, versus an 8-week audit plus a 12-week plan
with a written list of exclusions.
