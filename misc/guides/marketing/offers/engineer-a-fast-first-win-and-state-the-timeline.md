# Engineer a fast first win and state the timeline

**Feeds stage:** plan

Reduce time-to-value explicitly: build a small concrete first result into the
first seven days, front-load assets on day one, replace a slow kickoff with a
quick async intake, and quote a real end-to-end timeline. A stated short timeline
sells better, and a missed one permanently damages perceived likelihood for every
future buyer. For slow-cycle services, use a paid pilot with a day-30 milestone
instead of "trust us, results in six months."
