# Give the offer a repeatable name

**Feeds stage:** plan

A named offer beats an unnamed one: buyers can repeat it to a friend, it becomes
a distinct "thing" rather than a generic service, and it supports a higher price.
Name it for the outcome, the methodology, the identity it serves, or the
compressed timeframe. Test it with a text message: "I signed up for the [name],
it's $X and you get [one-line outcome]." Counterexample: generic "Marketing
Coaching Program" or internal jargon like "Tier 2 Standard" that no buyer can
repeat.
