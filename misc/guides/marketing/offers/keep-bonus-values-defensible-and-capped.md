# Keep stated bonus values defensible and capped

**Feeds stage:** plan

Give each bonus a stated value a buyer can verify against a comparable
standalone product, cap the total comfortably below 2x the core price (about
$1.5K of bonuses on a $1K offer), and keep the stack to 3–5 items — things you
would genuinely sell separately. Counterexample: "$50,000 in bonuses!" on a
$497 product; the asymmetry trips a sophisticated buyer's scam detector. If you
cannot point to a comparable price, drop the dollar amount.
