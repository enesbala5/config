# Keep the magnet consumable in under thirty minutes

**Feeds stage:** plan

The magnet should look worth paying for while demanding little time: consumable in
under 30 minutes, ideally under 10, with an immediate actionable takeaway. Density
beats length.

Diagnostic: when a long ebook converts at 4% on the landing page, suspect an effort
mismatch before rewriting the headline — 50 pages signals work. Counterexample:
three months spent on a 50-page ebook when a one-page checklist would deliver the
same first win. Chunk the ebook into a five-part email mini-course or split it into
topic-specific guides.
