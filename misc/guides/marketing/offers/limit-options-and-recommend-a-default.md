# Limit the options and recommend a default

**Feeds stage:** plan

Too many choices overwhelm and paralyse. Offer three tiers rather than seven, with
a clearly recommended "best for most" option, and pre-select that plan where the
choice architecture allows it. Defaults are powerful and should be used ethically
— the customer must still be able to choose differently. Counterexample: seven
similarly priced plans that force the buyer to do the comparison work.
