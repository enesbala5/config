# Make each bonus close a specific buying objection

**Feeds stage:** plan

A bonus's real job is to remove a named hesitation — "what if I get stuck?", "I
don't have time to implement this", "will my team buy in?". Build one bonus per
major objection instead of a pile of generic extras: a four-bonus stack that
closes four objections beats four "free!" extras. Find the objections by reading
refund requests and sales-call transcripts, reading your own page aloud, and
asking recent buyers "what almost stopped you buying?".
