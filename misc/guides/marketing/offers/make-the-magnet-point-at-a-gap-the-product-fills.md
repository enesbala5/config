# Make the magnet point at a gap the product fills

**Feeds stage:** plan

A magnet should solve a problem the product also solves, create awareness of the gap
the product closes, and demonstrate expertise in the space. That is what turns a
download into a path to adoption rather than a free file in a stranger's inbox.

Example: for project-management software sold to agencies, a project-profitability
calculator or capacity-planning template makes the exact gap visible. Counterexample:
a popular generic productivity checklist with no line from the problem to the
product — high downloads, no pipeline.
