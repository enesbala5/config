# Match the discount format to the price point

**Feeds stage:** plan

Under $100, a percentage discount sounds larger ("20% off" beats "$16 off" on an
$80 product); over $100, an absolute amount sounds larger ("$100 off" beats "20%
off" on a $500 product). The numbers are identical — only the frame changes. Pick
the format that makes the real saving clearest at this price point rather than
defaulting to one style for every offer. Use a discount only for a deliberate,
time-boxed acquisition push or a tested promo — never as a standing response to a
rival's price.
