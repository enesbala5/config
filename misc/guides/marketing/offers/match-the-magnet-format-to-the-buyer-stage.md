# Match the magnet format to the buyer's stage

**Feeds stage:** plan

Awareness leads need education, consideration leads need comparison and evaluation,
and decision leads need implementation help. Map the format to that job:

- Awareness — checklist, cheat sheet, ebook or quiz.
- Consideration — comparison template, maturity assessment, case study collection or
  webinar.
- Decision — ready-to-use template, free trial, migration/implementation checklist or
  ROI calculator.

Counterexample: a beginner checklist used to chase pipeline. It attracts people who
are not ready to buy, then gets blamed for low conversion when the mismatch is at
the format-and-stage level.
