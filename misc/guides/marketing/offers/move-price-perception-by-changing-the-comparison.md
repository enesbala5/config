# Move price perception by changing the comparison, not the number

**Feeds stage:** plan

Buyers judge price against the dream outcome, the next-best alternative, the cost
of doing nothing, and your own catalog — not against an absolute scale. You can
improve perceived value without changing the number: make the cost of inaction
vivid, anchor against a higher-priced option, and sequence higher-priced items
first. Counterexample: cutting the price when the real problem is that the buyer
has no reference point for what it is worth.
