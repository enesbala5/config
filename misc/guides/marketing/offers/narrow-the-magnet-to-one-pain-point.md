# Narrow the magnet to one pain point

**Feeds stage:** plan

A lead magnet should solve one clear problem, not cover a broad topic. Specificity
makes the promise credible and the asset quick to consume.

"How to write cold emails that get replies" beats "Marketing guide". Counterexample:
a 40-page "complete guide" that addresses everything and therefore delivers no
immediate win. Test the scope in one line: can the title name both the problem and
the outcome for a specific reader?
