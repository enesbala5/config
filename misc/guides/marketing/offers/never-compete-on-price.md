# Do not compete on price — cheapness itself signals low value

**Feeds stage:** plan

Justify a premium price with stacked value and guarantees rather than discounting
to match a cheaper rival; discounting signals low value. Costly signaling adds the
other half: a signal's persuasive strength is proportional to its cost, so what
looks "efficient" can communicate cheapness. This applies to list-price
competition; a deliberate, time-boxed, tested promo is a separate decision and
must be judged on its second-order effect. Counterexample: cutting price 40% to
answer a cheaper competitor.
