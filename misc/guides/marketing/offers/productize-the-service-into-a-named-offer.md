# Productize the service into a named, fixed offer

**Feeds stage:** plan

Turn "I sell consulting" into "I sell the 8-Week Marketing Reset": give the
service a name, a fixed scope and timeline, a fixed price, the same bonuses every
time, and a defined gate or review. Productizing raises perceived value,
simplifies selling, and creates a repeatable case-study factory. Counterexample:
an unnamed hourly service that competes on price because buyers cannot compare
it or repeat it to anyone.
