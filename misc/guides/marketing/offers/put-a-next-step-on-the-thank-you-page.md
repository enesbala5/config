# Put a next step on the thank-you page

**Feeds stage:** plan

After conversion, the thank-you page is still a live surface. Confirm delivery
("check your inbox"), then offer one next step: book a demo, start a trial, join the
community or read related content. A pre-written share post extends reach.

Counterexample: a bare "Thanks!" page that ends the relationship at the moment
intent is highest. Pick one next step, not a menu — the page should continue the
path the magnet started.
