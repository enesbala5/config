# Reverse the risk with a guarantee or trial

**Feeds stage:** plan

Let the product prove itself: a risk-reversing guarantee is a core part of a strong
offer, and a free sample or trial removes the buyer's perceived-likelihood risk.
Prefer proof the buyer can experience over claims you ask them to trust. Diagnostic:
what objection does the guarantee neutralise, and can the product demonstrate it?

The wrong guarantee hurts more than none, so match the type to the business:
first-time buyers need an unconditional refund, premium buyers read an
anti-guarantee as confidence, and sophisticated B2B expects an SLA or
pay-for-results. Keep conditions to two or fewer and stress-test what happens if
10% of buyers invoke it. Build proof first — strong proof with a weak guarantee
beats a strong guarantee with weak proof.
