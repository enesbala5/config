# Scarcity is the close, not the offer

**Feeds stage:** plan

Scarcity is the final lever, not the first fix. The order is a strong dream
outcome, specific likelihood (proof + method + guarantee), compressed time delay,
reduced effort, and only then a real deadline to force the decision. A strong
offer plus real scarcity makes the buyer decide now; a weak offer plus scarcity
makes them decide not to buy, faster. If conversions are bad, fix the value
equation before adding urgency.
