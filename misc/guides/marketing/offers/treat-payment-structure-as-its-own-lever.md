# Treat payment structure as its own lever

**Feeds stage:** plan

The same total price converts very differently depending on how it is paid — pay
in full, 2–4 installments, subscription, pay-after-results, or deposit plus
balance on delivery. When a price feels like a barrier, add a payment plan before
cutting the number: "$6K today" and "$2K x 3" are the same price to you but not
to the buyer. Pick the structure that fits buyer trust and your cash-flow
tolerance.
