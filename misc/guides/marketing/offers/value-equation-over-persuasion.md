# Strengthen the offer's value equation before the persuasion

**Feeds stage:** plan

Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay ×
Effort & Sacrifice). Find the weakest variable and fix that first. Offer strength
beats persuasion: a mediocre marketer with a great offer beats a great marketer
with a commodity one. Most "marketing" failures are offer or volume failures
upstream of the creative.
