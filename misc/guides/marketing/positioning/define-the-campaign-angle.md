# Define the campaign angle as one tension, insight or moment

**Feeds stage:** position

The campaign angle is the specific tension, insight or moment the whole campaign
lives in — not a slogan and not a list of benefits. State it in one line before any
deliverable is briefed, and pair it with two supporting artefacts:

- a core benefit statement: one sentence, no feature list, no jargon;
- the positioning formula: "[Product] helps [audience] [achieve outcome] by
  [mechanism]".

The angle is what makes otherwise unrelated pieces feel like one campaign. If you
cannot say which tension the work is dramatising, you have a collection of assets,
not a campaign.
