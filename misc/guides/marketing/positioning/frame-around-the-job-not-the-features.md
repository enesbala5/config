# Frame the product around the job, not its features

**Feeds stage:** position

People hire a product to get a job done. Lead with the outcome they want rather
than specifications: a drill buyer wants a hole, not a drill. Diagnostic: what
would the customer say they "hire" you for, and does your positioning say that?
Counterexample: a feature list that describes the product accurately but gives
the buyer no reason to care.
