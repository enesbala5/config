# Pick the market before the product, the product before the copy

**Feeds stage:** position

Leverage order is market > offer strength > persuasion. Find a starving crowd with
demonstrated buying behaviour before polishing the product or the copy; the
list/market is the single biggest success factor. Diagnostic: what have these
people already bought, how recently, how often, at what unit of sale? Counterexample:
the hamburger-stand exercise — better meat and a great location lose to a starving
crowd.
