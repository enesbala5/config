# Name the trade-off and the evidence that would settle it

**Feeds stage:** position

When expert lenses conflict, identify the underlying tension each conflict
represents (for example reach vs. resonance) and state what evidence would settle
it. Do not pick a side until you can state both positions in their strongest form —
steelman the opposing view rather than caricaturing it. A decision made without the
trade-off named is a preference, not a strategy.
