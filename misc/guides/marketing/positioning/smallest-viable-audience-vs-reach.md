# Decide smallest-viable-audience versus mass reach explicitly

**Feeds stage:** position

The central audience-priority conflict: delight the smallest viable audience
(Godin — "the relentless pursuit of mass will make you boring") versus reach all
category buyers, especially light and non-buyers (Sharp — growth comes from
penetration). Name which constraint binds this business and what evidence would
settle it. Counterexample to niche-first: double jeopardy — smaller brands have
fewer buyers and slightly lower loyalty, so tight targeting caps growth.
