# Use aggregated audience data for 'where', qualitative research for 'why'

**Feeds stage:** research

Tools like SparkToro answer where an audience spends time, what it reads and whom
it follows in seconds; they show behaviour, not language or emotion. Manual mining
of Reddit, G2 and communities gives you exact quotes and the "why". Use them
together: find the relevant podcasts, subreddits and sites with aggregated data,
then go into those sources manually for voice-of-customer language. Weight
behavioural data high-confidence, demographics medium, and never treat aggregated
data as a substitute for qualitative research.
