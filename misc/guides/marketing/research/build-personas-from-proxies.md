# With no first-party data, build personas outward from proxies

**Feeds stage:** research

Early-stage products lack reviews — do not invent personas. Walk outward in order:
(1) your own differentiator, written down as a hypothesis of who feels it most;
(2) direct competitors' reviews; (3) comparable products on marketplaces;
(4) adjacent brands that share the audience. Tag each proxy-built persona with its
proxy source and replace that evidence with first-party evidence as real reviews
arrive; treat them as provisional until then.
