# Capture exact customer words, not paraphrases

**Feeds stage:** research

Record desired outcomes and pains as verbatim quotes, not summaries — the
customer's own phrasing is the highest-value output of research because it is the
language buyers actually use. Counterexample: "manual process inefficiency"
throws away the signal in "we were drowning in spreadsheets." Keep a quote bank
indexed by theme so the original wording survives synthesis.
