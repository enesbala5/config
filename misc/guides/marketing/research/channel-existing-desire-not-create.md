# Channel existing desire — never try to create it

**Feeds stage:** research

Copy cannot create desire; it can only focus desires that already exist in the
market onto your product. Research the mass desire, hopes, fears and the market's
own words first, then build the message around what is already there. Trying to
manufacture want where none exists is the greatest marketing mistake — the market,
not the writer, writes the ad. Counterexample: the industry myth that "great copy
sells anything" to a crowd with no hunger.
