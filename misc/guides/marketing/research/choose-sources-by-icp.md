# Choose research sources by where your ICP talks, not where you are discussed

**Feeds stage:** research

Pick digital watering holes from the ICP type, not from where your own product is
already mentioned: B2B/technical → role subreddits, G2/Capterra, Hacker News,
LinkedIn; SMB founders → r/entrepreneur, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt; consumer →
1–3 star app-store reviews, hobby subreddits, social comments; enterprise →
LinkedIn, analyst reports, job postings. For a product category, start with
G2/Capterra reviews; for raw language use Reddit and YouTube comments; for
trigger events use LinkedIn posts, job postings and "Ask HN" threads.
