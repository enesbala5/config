# Establish the research mode before you start

**Feeds stage:** research

Decide first whether this is Mode 1 (you already have raw material — transcripts,
surveys, reviews, tickets — and need to extract signal) or Mode 2 (you need to go
find research in online communities, review sites and forums). Most engagements
combine both, but establish which applies before proceeding so you do not mine
sources you do not have or skip discovery you still need.
