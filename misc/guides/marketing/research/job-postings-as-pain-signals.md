# Treat job postings as admissions of pain

**Feeds stage:** research

A job posting is a company telling you what hurts: which tools are "required" vs
"nice to have" reveals the stack and adjacent tools, the metrics in the role
description reveal desired outcomes, and what the role spends most of its time on
reveals the job to be done. Search `site:linkedin.com/jobs "[role]" "[tool or
category]"` as a research source, not just a recruiting artefact.
