# Mine competitor four-star reviews for buried complaints

**Feeds stage:** research

A competitor's 4-star review is disproportionately valuable: the customer likes
the product but still has a complaint, so it reveals both strengths (battlecard
intel from "what do you like best?") and opportunities (from "what do you dislike?"
and "I wish it could…"). Use the "what problems are you solving?" field to capture
the job to be done, and note switching context and unmet needs.
