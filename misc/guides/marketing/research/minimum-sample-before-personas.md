# Require five independent data points before personas or messaging

**Feeds stage:** research

Do not build a persona or draw messaging conclusions from fewer than 5 independent
data points per segment (interviews, reviews or community posts). For personas the
source sets 5–10 from a consistent segment as the threshold. Below that, gather
more evidence; a persona built on two conversations is a guess wearing a persona
template. This is a floor for making a claim at all; a confidence label uses a
different rule (independent sources, prompted or unprompted, cross-segment
consistency), so a 5-point sample is not automatically High confidence.
