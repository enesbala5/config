# Qualify stage, resources and history before recommending ideas

**Feeds stage:** research

Before selecting from a tactic catalog, establish the product and audience, the
current stage and main growth goal, the budget and team size, what has already
been tried (and what worked or didn't), and which competitor tactics they admire.
These inputs are the filter: the same catalog yields a completely different
shortlist for a bootstrapped three-person team on $500/month than for an
enterprise with $10k/month.

Counterexample: answering "give me marketing ideas" with a generic list before
asking about stage or budget — every item is then untested against the
constraints that decide whether it can run at all.
