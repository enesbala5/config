# Cluster, then rank themes by frequency × intensity

**Feeds stage:** research

After extracting from individual assets, cluster similar pains, outcomes and
triggers into themes, then score each theme on how often it appears and how
strongly it is felt (emotional language). Rank by frequency × intensity, name 5–10
"money quotes" per theme, and explicitly flag contradictions — where customers say
one thing but do another. Raw frequency alone misses the themes that matter most.
