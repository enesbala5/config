# Read your own reviews in a deliberate order

**Feeds stage:** research

Mine your own product reviews in this order: 3-star first (most honest — they
stayed but something was missing), then 1-star (failure modes, separating product
issues from support/onboarding issues), then 5-star (the "what they love" proof
language), then 4-star (the buried "the only thing I wish…"). Reading them
chronologically or by rating loses the prioritisation.
