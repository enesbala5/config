# Segment before you average

**Feeds stage:** research

Split every analysis by customer tier, use case, role or tenure before drawing
conclusions. An average that spans segments hides the pattern you came to find.
For churn, separate the reasons — do not blend price, features, fit and timing
into one number. A persona that represents everyone represents no one; a single
"average customer" built from a power user and a churned trialist is a composite
that describes nobody.
