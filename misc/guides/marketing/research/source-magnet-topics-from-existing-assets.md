# Source magnet topics from existing assets and pre-purchase questions

**Feeds stage:** research

Before inventing a new asset, inventory what already exists: blog posts, guides,
data, internal templates and the expertise the team uses daily. Add the most common
question prospects ask before buying — a magnet that answers it is both specific and
naturally close to the product.

Example: a top-performing post on email subject lines can become a subject-line
template, a one-page cheat sheet or an annotated swipe file. Counterexample:
commissioning a broad "marketing guide" when the audience keeps asking one narrower
question the team could already answer.
