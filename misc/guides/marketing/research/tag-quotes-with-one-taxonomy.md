# Tag every quote with one shared taxonomy

**Feeds stage:** research

Use a single tag set across all sources — #pain, #trigger, #outcome, #language,
#alternative, #objection, #competitor — and keep a running table of Source | Date |
Quote | Tags | Notes. After 20–30 entries patterns emerge; quotes that recur across
unrelated sources are your highest-confidence insights. Without a common taxonomy,
findings from different sources cannot be compared.
