# clean means no artefacts, not that the sound is the thing

**Feeds stage:** building-sounds

The cookbook (`earshot recipe --list`) holds 99 recipes. 59 answer a request that names their thing on their own (a car crash, a cannon, a woodpecker, a fireball spell, an energy shield going up, marbles pouring); the other 40 are sketches that an answer names as a start. A recording of the thing itself answers first ("a rocket launch" is `rocket/launch`). `clean` means no clicks, no cut tail and no clipping — it does not mean the sound is the thing. Say what it is built from when you hand it over, and that a person should listen once.
