# Judge a build by fit, not by a good description

**Feeds stage:** building-sounds

Read `fit:` ("sounds like it" needs the fit and no contradiction from the second ear) and `heard as:`. The stampede answered `fit: z +3.72 for 'a stampede of horses' (the nearest design horse/neigh: z +2.61): sounds like it; recorded: p_real 0.97`, with `heard as: horse/neigh, wolf/howl, wind/window`. Applause is the counterexample: `earshot build applause` fits its words (z +4.69) but not 1.0 over the catalogue's own applause (z +4.50), so it prints "a sketch: not clearly it yet". The actual rule behind `fit`: "sounds like it" needs a clean sound, z of at least 3.0 and at least 1.0 above the design `make` would hand over, plus no objection from the second ear (the AudioSet tagger), which the tuner never sees — otherwise it prints "a sketch". In the stampede the second ear read `Neigh, whinny 0.92; Horse 0.71; Animal 0.57; Clip-clop 0.25`, and the heard-as words were `a horse neighing` (z +4.1), `a gust of wind rushing past` (z +3.2), `a cow mooing` (z +3.1). `fit` is a listening model's judgement and the second ear also objects to some real recordings: neither is a person's ear.
