# still off: names the field to edit

**Feeds stage:** building-sounds

Each `still off:` line names the field in `x.recipe.json` to change; then `earshot build "X" --recipe x.recipe.json` hears it again (`--rounds 20` tunes it again). In the stampede, 20 rounds moved `scatter.rate 16.5 -> 26.2`, `scatter.pitch 2 -> 1.33`, `scatter.gain 6 -> 9.4`, a duration 6 -> 5.04, and fit z +3.16 -> +3.72; the final `still off: nothing the measures find; a person should listen once`. A first try without "soft" on the rumble was "a sketch", and `still off` said the thunder covered the rest. `--without ID_OR_FAMILY` keeps parts out. A build loads the listening model (the listen extra) and takes about a minute; without the extra it drafts, renders, audits and measures, and says that it did not hear.
