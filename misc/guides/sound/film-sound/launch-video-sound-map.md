# The launch-video sound map: picture event, sound, frame

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

One decision table carries a launch film (picture → sound → frame): cursor moves, hovers, scrolls, progress and streaming text → nothing, the music carries it; click/tap → ONE real click voice for the film, press + release ~85 ms later, same level every time → the press frame; typing → one real keyboard, sliced, <= ~12 keys/s (one per word above ~25 chars/s), music dips 6-10 dB → each sounded character; a card/chip/badge → a soft real pop or tick climbing across a run → 50 % of its travel; toggle, lock-in, a counter's final value → a real latch/click/crisp tick → the contact/lock frame; camera-scale move, whip, zoom (~2 per film) → a real swish, apex on the velocity peak → the velocity peak; hard cuts → nothing; tension into the reveal → the music's build or a real riser ending 30-250 ms before the hit; hero reveal → the music's DROP, edited so its downbeat lands here, optionally one real impact → the frame; logo/lockup → music stops on a bar line or filters down, 0.2-0.7 s near-silence, ONE soft note in key, tail to silence → the frame the wordmark reads. Budget about 5 designed effects per 10 s and keep effects dry and centred.
