# The measured motion-effects palette

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

Stop before a reveal [measured, the most used move]: cut the music for 0.15 to 0.5 s, then come back on a hit — an edit of the bed, with drop/sack or hit/drop. Something appears or a card flips: a short low thump, about 50 ms [measured], thump/door (set's `pop`); in a run, make it once and place it once per element. A UI click or highlighted word: a bright dry tick [measured], tick/key (click/cursor is a whole mouse button). A title change: title/bag, a swish and breath into a heavy bag, about a third of a second. The logo end card: logo/bag, low, about a quarter second, on the logo frame; sting/reveal swells longer into a hit, impact/car heavier. A move: whoosh/fast (fast), whoosh/sweep (soft), slide/paper (card sliding in), flick/thin ("a quick swish"). Text breaking: glitch/short. Reveal after a build: rise/breath or rise/hit. A shine: shine/blade. Never as an effect in a motion video: marimba, bells, chimes, piano or synth notes [measured: none in 35 videos]; braams, explosions, tape stops and long risers were not used in any of them either — use them only when the picture shows that thing.
