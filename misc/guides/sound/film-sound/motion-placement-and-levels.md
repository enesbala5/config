# Place and level motion effects against the music

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

Where it sits [measured, few cases]: a hit starts about 80 ms before the picture changes and peaks about 60 ms after it; a whoosh peaks on the fastest frame [convention]. Levels against the music [measured]: hits about 18 dB over the music's median level at that moment, the hit after a stop and word ticks about 8 dB; near mono, dry. Measure the music at the cue (`earshot measure`, or `kit.bed_level` in the kit loop) and set the gain. Endings [measured]: most end at body level; end with the logo hit and the music's last note, no long silence.
