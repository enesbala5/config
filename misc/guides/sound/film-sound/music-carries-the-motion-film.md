# Music carries the film; effects are the exception

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

Measured on 39 reference motion-design videos: 34 of the 35 with sound are music-led. Where effects stand out of the music at all, a video has 1 to 4 of them. Pick the two to five moments that matter (a reveal, the logo, a key UI action, a title change); most cuts get no sound, because editors cut the picture on the music's accents. In a real-recordings launch film the same restraint is tighter: cursor moves, hovers, scrolls, progress and streaming text and hard cuts get nothing, with about 5 designed effects per 10 s, and the logo resolves on one soft note into a tail of silence. `earshot make --set film` makes these as one family; one alone uses `earshot make "WORDS" --sound ID`.
