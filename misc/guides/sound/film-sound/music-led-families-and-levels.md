# For music-cut films use the soft families and level to the bed

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

For music-led launch films prefer the families for films cut to music (`earshot sfx list --category ui_pop`, ...): `ui_pop` (rounded mid pops for chips, bubbles and rows arriving — the most used effect in those films), `knock` (mid wood/plastic), `cut_hit` (short sub hits with a kick), `mallet` (gated glockenspiel/celesta/marimba notes, soft pluck, chime runs; `--pitch` tunes them), `drip` (rising drops), `shutter` (snap zooms, whip-cuts), `reverse_swell` (peaks just after a cut, one per film), plus whoosh_big/thump*, riser dark/build/snatch, swish bright/flick, whoosh_fast whip, glitch burst/mid/chirp, notify mid_tine/mid_wood. Prefer them over the bright 5 ms blips and long cinematic tails of older categories. In films cut to music effects ride 3-10 dB over the music: a fixed `level_db` is buried in loud passages or sticks out in quiet ones. For each cue measure the bed and set the take from the lock (`kit.bed_level(music, t_cue)` = music's median 50 ms level the second before; `kit.gain_db(take, role, bed)`, never over -1 dBFS peak; `kit.place`; `kit.space_run(run_times, role['min_gap_ms'])` — pops 114 ms, keys 87 ms). Pull the music down 6-12 dB for 0.3-0.5 s before a hit, drop, big whoosh or swell and bring it back on the hit (`mix.stop_down`); do not duck under every effect. Without music `level_db` is the fallback; where music is silent before a cue `bed_level` returns `None` and `gain_db` uses `level_db`. Report the gains you set and the bed levels you measured.
