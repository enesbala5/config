# Score a real-footage scene thing by thing

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

Ask for each thing the picture shows as that thing, with its place or surface, and make them as one spec for a first balance: "steady rain on a terrace, 8 seconds, no loop" (rain/street), "the heavy wooden front door falls shut" (door/shut), "keys jingling" (keys/bunch), "a phone vibrating on a table" (phone/vibrate), "distant thunder" (thunder/distant), "footsteps on concrete, 4 seconds" (step/concrete, a walk). Read what the picked design is before it goes under a picture: "a kitchen timer ringing" answers `timer/done`, an app's marimba timer figure, not a wind-up bell. Leave a moment silent, record it, or build it rather than use a stand-in; a thing the catalogue has no recording of is better recorded, built or left silent than replaced. One bed for the place, then the real sound of each shown thing.
