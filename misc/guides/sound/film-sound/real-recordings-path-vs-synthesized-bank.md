# Choose the real-recordings path when the film needs recorded sound

**Feeds stage:** film-sound

The real-sound path builds a soundtrack ONLY from real, professionally recorded or produced audio: one licensed music track, real foley, real SFX and real ambiences. Code selects, trims, places, levels, pans, EQs, pitches by resampling, time-stretches the music and masters; code never generates a sound. Choose this path when the brief asks for recorded sound, a licensed music edit, or a film where every sound is caused by something on screen; the bank/forge path is synthesized in code and is not a substitute when recordings are required. The bar is a professional launch film: one material palette, the music's drop on the hero reveal, restraint everywhere else, a resolved logo and a tail into silence.
