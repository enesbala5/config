# Open the sheet image and fix the critical critique rows

**Feeds stage:** listening

`earshot sheet film.mp4 -o sheet.png --cues cues.json` (`--from 10 --to 25` zooms, `--detail low` is faster) makes an annotated PNG of frames, sections, labelled events, spectrogram, loudness, beats and cue markers coloured by sync: green within a frame, amber within 100 ms, red no sound. OPEN THE PNG with your image reader — gaps, pile-ups, masking and late hits are obvious at a glance. Then `earshot critique film.mp4` (`--fast` skips AudioSet/realness) gives a scorecard against the measured range of professional launch films: each metric ok/low/high with its weight, actions with timestamps, and a verdict (launch-grade / close / needs work / not launch-grade). Fix critical rows first and re-run the timeline/sheet/critique on the new render. Its "onset on visual events" share counts new attacks within +-100 ms only; fixes name cuts with no sound at all. If the file is one of the profile's reference films, critique says so and scores it against the others. For exact numbers use `earshot measure mix.wav [--music]` (MCP `measure`): LUFS, true peak dBTP, LRA, noise floor, attack/decay, brightness, bands. Validation: each reference film critiqued against the other six falls in range on 86 % of metrics (a no-overfit baseline is about 71 %). Build your own reference profile from films you admire with `earshot profile REFS... -o p.json`.
