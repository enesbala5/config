# Describe the sound, not its job

**Feeds stage:** listening

Write queries as source + material + action + character: "a heavy wooden door slamming in a small room", not "a sound for the logo". Give alternatives that are realistic confusions (thock vs click vs typewriter). When a z is borderline, try two or three phrasings and trust a reading that holds up under all of them. Job words are not heard: "a soft rounded pop, something appearing" passed none of 9 real pops. To check a sound for a job, describe the job's sound two or three ways and keep the best reading.
