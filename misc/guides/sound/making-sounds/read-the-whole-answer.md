# Read the whole answer, not just the file

**Feeds stage:** making-sounds

The answer is the whole result; ask for `--json` (MCP `full=true`) only for measurements. `x.ogg: design/id (0.88 s, peak at 0.05 s, G5, marimba, -14 LUFS, clean) [words]` gives the design, length, loudest moment, note and voice, loudness, and `clean` or what the audit found — report this line. Anything other than `clean` is not usable: remake with another `seed`. `also: a, b, c` lists alternatives; if the id does not fit the job, call again with `--sound ID`. `also (the pick is a guess: ...)` means unverified: choose by an alternative's words or ask again naming the sound or the job. Other lines to act on: `not in this sound: WORDS` — part of the request is absent (a second thing wants its own call, a verb/adjective of the same thing does not); `not followed: ...` or `0.43 s, not the 2 s asked` — no such control or length, and the line says what happened ("from left to right" is never followed: earshot does not pan); `a cut of 4 s, not a loop`, `a seamless loop of 8.00 s`, `8.57 s: a music loop is whole bars` — say "a seamless loop of ..." when it must repeat; `not understood: WORDS` — those words did nothing, rephrase if they mattered; `same design as NAME` (two names got one sound) — give the second `"sound": ID`; a loop in mp3 has a gap at its seam — write that loop as ogg, webm, wav or flac.
