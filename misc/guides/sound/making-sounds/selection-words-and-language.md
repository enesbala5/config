# Which words select a musical or synthesized sound

**Feeds stage:** making-sounds

Interface and motion requests get normal sounds. Notes on an instrument answer only when the words ask for music (an instrument, chime, jingle, melody) or a theme is named; naming an instrument sets the voice ("a notification on a harp" → marimba, harp, pizzicato, felt_piano, kalimba). Chip sound only with "8-bit", "retro" or "chiptune"; a bell or glass timbre only when asked for one. With a theme named (soft, glass, wood, felt, tine, metal, electric, digital, retro), the `ui` set plays tonal figures in that voice instead of mechanical sounds. Requests are English: German is read through a table of common sound words ("Regen auf Holz" is rain on wood); other languages get "write it in English".
