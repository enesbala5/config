# Say what a made sound is and what was not checked

**Feeds stage:** placement-and-limits

Do not say how a made sound sounds beyond its descriptions. What validation actually found: no listening model predicted how a person rated the made sounds; a maintainer judged them by ear on listening pages that were not blind. Takes of learned recordings were judged as good as the recordings themselves, and chip sound, soft tonal figures, rain on a window and the forest bed passed. Sounds invented from formulas for real things (footsteps, punches, explosions, doors), most music beds, drums invented from physics and bell or glass interface timbres failed — which is why realistic sounds are learned from recordings now. The sounds built last (the `ui` set, film and motion hits, newer learned things and beds, recipes and builds) have been measured but not judged by anyone. Say plainly that a learned sound is a new take of a public-domain recording, not a sound invented from nothing. Everything earshot renders is free to use, commercially too, without credit. If realness was unavailable, say real-vs-synthetic was not checked; if a trim is labeled a sketch, hand it over as a sketch and say a person should listen once.
