# Most moments get no sound

**Feeds stage:** placement-and-limits

A sound belongs where something happened that a person should notice without looking. A page or app: sound for what the user did on purpose and what came of it (switch thrown, form sent, payment gone through, error, message arriving), none for what happens by itself or all the time (page loading, scrolling, animation, pointer crossing links). A landing page needs three to six of the `ui` roles, not all 17; `hover` is for tools where hovering is a choice. A game: every repeated action gets a short sound in several takes (steps, jumps, hits, coins), rewards and level-end get longer instrument figures, a menu gets one small sound for moving and one for choosing. A motion video: two to five effects in a film that music leads. A real-footage scene: one bed for the place plus the real sound of each shown thing.
