# What the tooling cannot do or hear

**Feeds stage:** placement-and-limits

No music beyond three short loops (`bed/warm`, `bed/playful`, `bed/8bit`) — earshot is for sound effects. No speech, singing or melodies; instruments play single notes and short figures. `make` does not pan (a recipe layer can). No realistic sound for a thing nobody recorded except what a build makes of other recordings. No speech recognition (it can tell there is speech, not what is said), no lyric or language judgement. CLAP hears 50 Hz - 14 kHz and AST up to 8 kHz: check "air" and low-pass problems with `measure` (bandwidth_hz, bands). Very short sounds are padded to a 10 s window; isolated hits read best with find/describe on a trimmed segment. `attack_ms`/`decay_ms` come from smoothed 5 ms/10 ms RMS envelopes: an instant attack reads ~4 ms and short clicks cannot read below ~10-20 ms of decay. Picture cuts come from frame-difference detection: hard cuts and whips are reliable, slow crossfades missed or late, motion inside a shot is not an event — pass cue times (`--cues`) for animation. Mixed dense material blurs tags: describe a stem or segment, not the whole mix. What earshot can judge: loudness, timing and "is this the kind of sound I asked for" on single sounds are trustworthy; tags inside a busy mix, genre and mood are hints; taste, emotion, licences and speech content it cannot judge. `build` is not a way to any sound: applause stays a sketch, and rain or hail on a new surface, a vehicle passing built from another motor, voices and pure tones do not build.
