# The listening models judge; they do not generate audio

**Feeds stage:** tooling-and-providers

No model generates audio in earshot. `make` renders catalogue designs, `build` and recipes lay out recorded parts, and the bank, forge and kit engines synthesise from code; CLAP, the AudioSet tagger and the model-free measurements only judge the result. Treat a model score as a critic, not as evidence the sound got better: the forge search optimises earshot's own models, and `build`'s `fit` is a listening model's judgement, not a person's ear. When a model is missing or a check could not be run, say plainly what was not heard rather than implying the audio was validated.
