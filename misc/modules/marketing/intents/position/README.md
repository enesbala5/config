# Intent: position

Owns the decision about the problem to own, the audience priority, category,
differentiation, campaign angle, messaging hierarchy and claims/anti-claims.
Trigger: "what should we say / how do we position this / who is this for" after the
research exists.

## Read first

`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/AGENTS.md` owns the hard gates. Then read, in
order: `<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/positioning/README.md`, the guide
index `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/README.md`, and the research
brief. Do not load a whole bucket or a sibling intent.

## Sequence

1. Confirm the research brief, product facts, audience priority and the human's
   approval of what may be claimed.
2. Choose smallest-viable audience or reach explicitly; name the status-quo
   competitor; decide whether to use or create a category.
3. Frame around the job, then define one campaign angle with its benefit statement
   and positioning formula.
4. Write claims and anti-claims; test each message against them.
5. Record the approved positioning brief and tone profile before any handoff.

## Gates

- Claims and anti-claims are explicit; no unsupported outcome guarantee.
- The angle is approved before copy is briefed.
- No visual or layout direction is produced.

## Rules applied

`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/positioning/README.md` (evidence in
`<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/`), plus the relevant limits in
`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/mistakes/README.md`.

## Handoff

- On success → **plan** to turn the position into an offer and channel plan, or
  **handoff** when the next step is copy.
- On failure or out of scope → **research** for missing evidence; do not invent a
  position.

## Never

- Write copy, choose layouts/colours/type, or leave claims implicit.
