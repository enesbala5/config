# Intent: research

Owns requests that need evidence before a decision: audience, ICP, VOC, pain
points, competitors, demand, personas or a research brief. Trigger: "who is this
for / what do they struggle with / what does the market look like" — not "write the
page".

## Read first

`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/AGENTS.md` owns the hard gates. Then read, in
order: `<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/research/README.md`, the guide index
`<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/README.md`, and only the named lessons.
Do not load a whole bucket or a sibling intent.

## Sequence

1. Confirm the goal, research mode, deliverable and what data or project context
   already exists.
2. Read existing project marketing context before asking the human.
3. Choose sources by ICP; capture verbatim; segment before averaging.
4. Cluster and rank themes by frequency × intensity; label confidence; enforce the
   five-point floor before personas or messaging.
5. Close with an exploitable brief that names the insights and the evidence behind
   each.

## Gates

- Goal, mode, deliverable and sample threshold are confirmed before conclusions.
- No persona field is invented; proxies are named.
- Every insight carries a confidence label.

## Rules applied

`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/research/README.md` (evidence in
`<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/`), plus the evidence rules in
`<config>/misc/modules/marketing/rules/mistakes/README.md`.

## Handoff

- On success → **position** with the research brief, or **handoff** when the request
  is a VOC quote bank for `copy`.
- On failure or out of scope → ask the human for the missing input; do not proceed
  on assumption.

## Never

- Write copy, pick the campaign angle, choose channels, or present a conclusion
  below the evidence floor.
