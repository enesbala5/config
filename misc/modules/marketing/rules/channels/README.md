# Channel rules

What must be true when choosing where the work happens.

## Choose a few channels by signal, budget and time horizon

**Must be true:** Channels are selected from audience signal and filtered by budget
and time horizon, not chosen because they are familiar.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/choose-channels-by-the-signal-the-audience-gives.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/filter-channels-by-budget.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/match-tactic-to-time-horizon.md`

## Route every channel back to owned capture

**Must be true:** Every channel routes attention to a list, community or account the
business controls.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/route-every-channel-back-to-owned.md`

## Prefer always-on reach over one-off bursts

**Must be true:** Reach is built continuously and scaled to the size of the update,
not concentrated in a single launch spike.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/always-on-reach-over-bursts.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/scale-announcement-to-update-size.md`

## Earn permission rather than buy interruption

**Must be true:** The plan prefers opted-in, anticipated contact over rented or
interruptive reach.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/permission-over-interruption.md`

## Split effort between proven and experimental channels

**Must be true:** Most effort goes to proven channels and a bounded share to small
experiments; effort is doubled only where there is a real edge.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/split-budget-between-proven-and-experimental.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/double-down-where-you-have-an-edge.md`

## Community is built on shared identity and member value

**Must be true:** A community is organised around a shared identity, on a platform
the business can own, with seeded conversations, one rewarded core loop, and value
flowing to members first.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/build-community-around-shared-identity.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/choose-community-platform-by-audience-and-ownership.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/seed-conversations-before-opening.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/define-and-reward-one-weekly-core-loop.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/make-value-flow-to-members-first.md`

## Budget community management as a real role

**Must be true:** Community work has an owner and weekly time budget before the
community opens.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/budget-community-management-as-a-role.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/invest-in-the-one-percent-who-create-value.md`

## Match the capture gate to the funnel stage

**Must be true:** The amount asked at opt-in matches the visitor's stage; ask for the
minimum, deliver by email, and prefer a post-specific content upgrade over gating
the post.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/match-the-gate-to-the-funnel-stage.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/ask-for-the-minimum-and-qualify-later.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/deliver-by-email-to-verify-and-start-the-relationship.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/channels/make-a-post-specific-content-upgrade-instead-of-gating-the-post.md`
