# Handoff rules

What must be true at the boundary between strategy and execution.

## Strategy decides; copy and design produce

**Must be true:** Strategy owns the decision and the brief; `copy` owns the words;
`design` owns the visual and layout execution. No role silently takes another's work.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/strategy-decides-direction-execution-produces.md`

## Gate copy on an approved positioning brief and tone

**Must be true:** Copy is briefed only after the positioning brief, campaign angle
and tone profile are approved.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/gate-copy-on-approved-positioning.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/lock-tone-before-production.md`

## Deliver a quote bank or research brief before copy

**Must be true:** Research is handed off as a VOC quote bank or brief, not as a
request to write the copy.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/quote-bank-then-copy.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/hand-off-research-to-downstream-specialists.md`

## Testing designs the test; copy writes the variants

**Must be true:** Experiment design and copy variant production are separate jobs,
with the test owner naming the variable and the copy owner writing it.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/ab-testing-designs-copy-writes-variants.md`

## Hand off a brief, not a finished asset

**Must be true:** Magnets, campaigns and programmes are handed over as briefs with
aims and constraints, not as finished copy or visuals.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/hand-off-a-magnet-brief-not-a-finished-asset.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/name-the-idea-defer-the-program-design.md`

## Gate cross-deliverable consistency

**Must be true:** Before production, the set of deliverables is checked for a
consistent angle, claims and tone.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/gate-on-cross-deliverable-consistency.md`

## Every recommendation states fit, steps, outcome and resources

**Must be true:** A strategy recommendation names who it fits, the first steps, the
expected outcome and the resources required.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/state-fit-steps-outcome-and-resources.md`

## Psychology informs hypotheses; experiments settle them

**Must be true:** A psychological explanation is framed as a hypothesis and handed
to experiment design, never asserted as proof.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/handoffs/psychology-informs-hypotheses-ab-testing-designs.md`
