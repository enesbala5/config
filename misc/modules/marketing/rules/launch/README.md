# Launch rules

What must be true before, during and after a launch.

## Clear the pre-launch gate before announcing

**Must be true:** Messaging, landing surface, activation path and one acquisition
channel are ready before any announcement goes out.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/clear-the-pre-launch-gate.md`

## Sequence tactics by stage and validate each phase

**Must be true:** Tactics run in dependency order, and each phase is validated before
the next expands.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/sequence-tactics-by-stage.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/validate-each-phase-before-expanding.md`

## Give every deliverable one job in the campaign arc

**Must be true:** Each deliverable has a single named job, and the content layers are
ordered so each informs the next.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/give-every-deliverable-one-job.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/order-content-layers-by-dependency.md`

## Run launch day as an operations event

**Must be true:** Launch day has scheduled touchpoints, staffed real-time response,
issue monitoring, and every channel routed to owned capture.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/run-launch-day-as-an-operations-event.md`

## Prepare platform launches weeks ahead and follow up after

**Must be true:** A platform launch has 2–4 weeks of preparation and a follow-up plan
that converts the spike into owned relationships.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/treat-a-platform-launch-as-a-prepared-all-day-event.md`

## Plan the post-launch adoption phase

**Must be true:** The plan covers adoption after the announcement, not only the
announcement itself.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/plan-the-post-launch-adoption-phase.md`

## Launch repeatedly and scale to update size

**Must be true:** Launches are repeatable events, and announcement effort scales to
the size of the update.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/launch-again-and-again.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/scale-announcement-to-update-size.md`

## Keep a new community closed until baseline activity exists

**Must be true:** A new community opens only after seeded baseline activity is
visible.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/launch/keep-a-new-community-closed-until-active.md`
