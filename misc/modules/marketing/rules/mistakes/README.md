# Mistake rules

Anti-patterns and ethical limits that every intent must avoid.

## Never invent persona or evidence fields

**Must be true:** Persona fields and research gaps are left blank or marked
unknown; no detail is fabricated to complete a template.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/do-not-invent-persona-details.md`

## Check sample bias before generalising

**Must be true:** Before saying "all customers", the sample's over-representation and
survivorship are checked.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/check-sample-bias.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/study-the-failures-not-just-the-viral-hit.md`

## Check the second-order effect before scaling a lever

**Must be true:** A tactic is judged by the behaviour it teaches on repeat, not by
its first-order spike.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/check-the-second-order-effect.md`

## Use scarcity only when the constraint is real

**Must be true:** No false deadline, fake limit or invented urgency is used.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/use-scarcity-only-when-the-constraint-is-real.md`

## Diagnose a launch flop as a pre-launch gap first

**Must be true:** A weak launch is diagnosed against the pre-launch gate before the
channel or the audience is blamed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/diagnose-a-launch-flop-as-a-pre-launch-gap.md`

## Do not cherry-pick results after the fact

**Must be true:** Segments and success criteria are pre-registered; no post-hoc
winner is presented as the result.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/pre-register-segments-before-slicing.md`

## Rewrite copy or messaging that fits a competitor unchanged

**Must be true:** Any message that would work unchanged for an unrelated brand is
treated as a default, not a position.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/rewrite-copy-that-fits-a-competitor.md`

## Separate the model from the customer

**Must be true:** When the model and the customer disagree, the process is examined
before the customer is blamed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/separate-the-model-from-the-customer.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/examine-the-process-before-blaming-the-customer.md`

## Curate ideas by fit; never dump a catalog

**Must be true:** Ideas are filtered by stage, resources and evidence, and the
catalog is not presented as a plan.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/curate-ideas-not-catalog-dump.md`

## Match the decision process to the stakes

**Must be true:** The size of the advisory or review process matches the stakes; a
council that only agrees is treated as a mirror, not validation.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/match-bench-to-stakes.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/agreeing-council-is-a-mirror.md`

## Judge a lead magnet against its stage's job

**Must be true:** A beginner magnet is not called broken when it is doing its stage's
job; formats are not mixed in one magnet.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/do-not-call-a-beginner-magnet-broken.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/do-not-mix-formats-in-one-lead-magnet.md`

## Treat company-dominated community posts as red flags

**Must be true:** A community where the company dominates the posts and questions go
unanswered is not reported as healthy.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/treat-company-dominated-posts-as-red-flags.md`
