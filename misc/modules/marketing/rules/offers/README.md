# Offer rules

What must be true of the offer itself, before any persuasion.

## Fix the weakest value-equation variable first

**Must be true:** The offer is strengthened by improving the weakest of outcome,
likelihood, time delay or effort before more persuasion is added.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/value-equation-over-persuasion.md`

## Change one offer lever per iteration and project lift as a range

**Must be true:** One offer lever changes at a time, and expected lift is stated as
a range with no 5x promise.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/project-offer-lift-honestly.md`

## Do not match a rival's price

**Must be true:** A premium is justified with stacked value and guarantees; a
deliberate, time-boxed, tested promo is a separate decision.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/never-compete-on-price.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/match-discount-format-to-price-point.md`

## Guarantees have defined conditions and limits

**Must be true:** Every guarantee or trial states its scope, conditions and stress
test before it is offered.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/reverse-the-risk-with-a-guarantee.md`

## Limit options and recommend a default

**Must be true:** The offer presents a small set of options with one clear default,
not an open menu.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/limit-options-and-recommend-a-default.md`

## State scope, payment structure and timeline in writing

**Must be true:** The offer names its scope, payment structure, first win and
timeline explicitly.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/define-the-scope-explicitly-in-writing.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/treat-payment-structure-as-its-own-lever.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/engineer-a-fast-first-win-and-state-the-timeline.md`

## Scarcity is a close, used only when the constraint is real

**Must be true:** Scarcity is added at the close and only when the stated limit
genuinely exists.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/scarcity-is-the-close-not-the-offer.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/use-scarcity-only-when-the-constraint-is-real.md`

## Bonuses close specific objections and stay defensible

**Must be true:** Each bonus answers a named buying objection, its stated value is
defensible and capped, and bonuses add to a strong core.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/make-each-bonus-close-a-specific-objection.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/keep-bonus-values-defensible-and-capped.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/bonuses-add-to-a-strong-core-not-a-patch.md`

## A lead magnet serves one pain, one format, one next step

**Must be true:** The magnet addresses one pain point, uses one format, is
consumable in under thirty minutes, and names the next step.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/narrow-the-magnet-to-one-pain-point.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/do-not-mix-formats-in-one-lead-magnet.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/offers/put-a-next-step-on-the-thank-you-page.md`
