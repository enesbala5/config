# Positioning rules

What must be true before a market position, angle or message is approved.

## Choose smallest-viable audience or mass reach explicitly

**Must be true:** The audience strategy is a stated choice, with its trade-off
named, rather than an assumed default.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/smallest-viable-audience-vs-reach.md`

## Treat the status quo as the main competitor

**Must be true:** The position explains why the audience should change from what
they do today, not only why the product beats a named rival.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/status-quo-is-the-competitor.md`

## Default to an existing category

**Must be true:** An existing category is used unless evidence justifies creating a
new one; category creation is the named exception.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/category-creation-is-the-exception.md`

## Decide market, then product, then copy

**Must be true:** The market and product position are settled before any copy or
campaign angle is briefed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/market-before-product-before-copy.md`

## Frame around the job, not the features

**Must be true:** The position is written around the job the audience is trying to
do, not a feature inventory.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/frame-around-the-job-not-the-features.md`

## Build distinctive assets, not claimed differentiation

**Must be true:** Differentiation rests on recognisable assets or behaviour, not an
adjective the competitor could also claim.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/distinctive-assets-over-differentiation.md`

## Lead with the mechanism when claims wear out

**Must be true:** When a category's claims are exhausted, the message leads with how
the product works.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/mechanism-when-claims-wear-out.md`

## State claims and anti-claims explicitly

**Must be true:** The position records what may be claimed and what may not, and
naming the trade-off and the evidence that would settle it.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/name-the-tradeoff-and-its-evidence.md`

## Define one campaign angle before briefing deliverables

**Must be true:** The campaign angle is one tension, insight or moment stated in one
line, with the benefit statement and positioning formula, before any deliverable is
briefed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/define-the-campaign-angle.md`

## Align with an existing audience belief

**Must be true:** The message connects to something the audience already believes
rather than asking them to adopt a new worldview.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/positioning/align-with-existing-beliefs.md`
