# Research rules

What must be true when gathering marketing evidence.

## Establish the goal and research mode before analysis

**Must be true:** The goal, the research mode, and the available data are stated
before any data is analysed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/establish-research-mode-first.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/ask-goal-before-analysis.md`

## Read existing product-marketing context before asking the human

**Must be true:** Existing project context is read first; the human is asked only
for what it does not answer.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/read-context-before-asking.md`

## Segment before averaging

**Must be true:** Evidence is split into meaningful segments before any average,
theme or conclusion is stated.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/segment-before-averaging.md`

## Capture customer language verbatim

**Must be true:** Quotes and customer phrases are recorded exactly; paraphrase is
never presented as evidence.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/capture-verbatim-language.md`

## Rank themes by frequency and intensity

**Must be true:** Themes are ranked by both how often and how strongly they appear,
not by frequency alone.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/rank-themes-by-frequency-and-intensity.md`

## No persona or messaging conclusion below five independent data points

**Must be true:** A persona or messaging conclusion has at least five independent
data points per segment; below that, more evidence is gathered.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/minimum-sample-before-personas.md`

## Label every insight with a confidence level

**Must be true:** Each finding carries High / Medium / Low confidence with the
explicit criteria used, and the label is stated before the claim.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/label-insights-with-confidence.md`

## Weight recent evidence most heavily

**Must be true:** The last twelve months carry the most weight; older evidence is
marked as historical.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/weight-recent-evidence.md`

## Choose sources by where the ICP talks

**Must be true:** Research sources are chosen by where the target audience actually
talks, including competitor reviews and job postings, not by where the company is
discussed.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/choose-sources-by-icp.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/mine-competitor-four-star-reviews.md`

## Close research with an exploitable brief

**Must be true:** Research ends with a brief that names the insights to exploit and
the evidence behind each, not a data dump.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/close-research-with-an-exploitable-brief.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/ask-which-deliverable.md`

## Proxies over invented detail

**Must be true:** With no first-party data, personas are built outward from named
proxies and unknown fields are left blank.

Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/research/build-personas-from-proxies.md`, `<config>/misc/guides/marketing/mistakes/do-not-invent-persona-details.md`
