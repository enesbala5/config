# Intent: place

Owns the decision of whether a moment gets sound at all, and what may be said
about it. Trigger: "should this have a sound" or "what can I claim about this" —
the restraint and honesty gate before `make` or `score`.

## Read first

`<config>/misc/modules/sound/AGENTS.md` owns the hard gates. Then, in order:
`<config>/misc/modules/sound/rules/evidence/README.md`, the guide root
`<config>/misc/guides/sound/README.md`, and the named bucket `placement-and-limits/`.

## Sequence

1. Ask whether something happened that a person should notice without looking.
   Passive and continuous events get no sound.
2. Choose restraint: a few sounds that matter, not every available role.
3. State what the sound is, what was checked, and what was not. Hand a sketch
   over as a sketch.
4. If a written second opinion would upload audio, ask for consent first.
5. Send the resulting sound needs to `make` or `score`.

## Gates

- Most moments stay silent; sound marks an event, not ambient busyness.
- No quality claim beyond the sound's descriptions; taste and emotion are not
  presented as measured.
- Upload consent is explicit before any external critique.

## Rules applied

`<config>/misc/modules/sound/rules/evidence/README.md` — driven by the
`placement-and-limits` bucket.

## Handoff

- A sound is warranted → `make`, or `score` for a film.
- Nothing is warranted → done, with the silence decision recorded.

## Never

- Add sound to loading, scrolling, motion or other self-running events, or imply
  a person's taste was validated by a model.
