# Rules: evidence

What may be claimed, on what reading, and when to stay silent. Driven by the
`listening` and `placement-and-limits` buckets.

- No claim about how audio sounds, or that it fits, exists without a quoted
  earshot reading.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/never-claim-how-it-sounds-without-a-reading.md`
- Exact measurements outrank verdicts, `z` and probabilities; raw similarity is
  never presented as confidence.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/reading-the-numbers.md`
- A model score is a critic, not proof of improvement. Judge closeness to the
  references and ask for a blind test before calling a made sound as good as real.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/goodhart.md`
- Realness is judged on the exact trim that will be used, not on a convenient
  full-file pass.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/realness-thresholds-and-trim.md`
- A/B, timeline and critique results confirm each other before a fix is claimed.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/ab-match-compare.md`,
  `<config>/misc/guides/sound/listening/timeline-and-sync.md`
- State what was not checked and cannot be judged; a sketch is handed over as a
  sketch, and taste is never reported as a measurement.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/placement-and-limits/honesty-about-made-sounds.md`,
  `<config>/misc/guides/sound/placement-and-limits/what-not-to-expect-and-perceptual-limits.md`,
  `<config>/misc/guides/sound/placement-and-limits/taste-is-not-measured.md`
- An external written critique runs only with explicit per-call upload consent.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/placement-and-limits/llm-second-opinion-consent.md`
- Most moments get no sound: sound marks something a person would notice without
  looking, not passive or continuous activity.
  Evidence: `<config>/misc/guides/sound/placement-and-limits/most-moments-get-no-sound.md`
